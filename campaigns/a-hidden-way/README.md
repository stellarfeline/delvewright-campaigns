# A Hidden Way

> **Requires delve engine 0.62.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.13.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

The keeper's hall at Ashby has a great hearth whose fire has burned for three
generations. The valve that floods its flue is on the wall beside it. Turn the
valve, and watch the hearth.

A demo level of five minutes or so, for one to four players. No fight.

## Classes

- **Keeper's Hand** — a sword, boots and bread. The keeper sent for a hand to
  turn the old valve and put out the great hearth.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → the address the host gives you.
Accept the resource pack when the server offers it.

## For the owner: what to look for

This demo level shows one thing: a way the player is not meant to know of
stays unseen and unread-about until the close-up shot that opens it, and the
shot is what tells it. Walk it once as the hand.

1. **Before the valve: unseen.** Look at the great hearth from anywhere in the
   hall — close up, from the door, from the corners. Does it read as a
   fireplace and nothing else, with no glimpse of anything behind its back?
2. **Before the valve: unread-about.** Read everything the level shows you
   before you turn the valve — the class you pick, the line on arriving, the
   objective and its hint. Does any of it tell you, or hint, that there is
   something behind the hearth?
3. **The shot tells it.** Turn the valve. A short close-up shot shows the
   hearth as the fire goes out and its back swings away. Is the shot what tells
   you there is a way behind the hearth — and does it tell you plainly?

The machine has already confirmed: while the way is shut, no place you can
stand has a line of sight through anything see-through into the passage — the
firebox's back is a block the game draws as solid; the beat that opens it
carries the shot of its mouth; and no line you can read before the shot names
the passage or where it is, in English or in Chinese. What you judge is
whether it looks and reads that way.

## 给主人看的：要看什么

这个演示关卡只展示一件事：一条不想让玩家事先知道的路，在打开它的那个特写镜头之前，既看不见、也读不到；告诉玩家的，是那个镜头。以帮手的身份走一遍即可。

1. **拧阀门之前：看不见。** 从大厅里任何地方看那座大壁炉——凑近看、从门口看、从角落看。它读起来是不是就是一个壁炉，从它的背后看不到任何东西？
2. **拧阀门之前：读不到。** 把拧阀门之前关卡给你看的所有文字都读一遍——你选的职业、进门时的那句话、任务目标和提示。有没有哪一句告诉你、或者暗示你，壁炉后面有东西？
3. **镜头告诉你。** 拧开阀门。一个短短的特写镜头看向壁炉：火灭了，炉膛的砖背转开。是不是这个镜头告诉了你壁炉后面有一条路——而且说得清清楚楚？

机器已经确认：路关着的时候，你能站的任何地方都没有一条视线能穿过任何透光的东西看进密道——炉膛的砖背是游戏画成实心的方块；打开它的那一段剧情里有对着它入口的镜头；镜头之前你能读到的任何一句话，英文和中文都算，都没有提到密道或者它在哪儿。你要判断的只是它看起来、读起来是不是这样。
