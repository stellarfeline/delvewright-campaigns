# Three Ways Shut

> **Requires delve engine 0.59.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.13.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

The toll yard at Ashford Cut has been shut since the floods. Every way out of
it is closed in its own fashion: the arch to the inner court is barred where
everyone can see it, the toll house door is bolted from its own side, the
keeper's hatch on the ledge is locked, and the old cart road behind the yard's
fireplace has not been walked in years. The keeper left a key in the chest at
the end of the passage. Fetch it, open the cart road, and raise the bars on the
arch from the toll house.

A demo level of ten minutes or so, for one to four players. No fight.

## Classes

- **Toll Runner** — a sword, boots and bread. The toll company sends a runner
  when a yard is shut too long, and this season it is you.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → the address the host gives you.
Accept the resource pack when the server offers it.

## For the owner: what to look for

This demo level shows that every way the story keeps shut says how the player
learns it is shut. The yard has three shut ways, one for each way of learning,
and a long cart road built the right way beside them. Walk it once as the toll
runner; there is no hurry.

1. **The arch you see at once.** Coming into the yard, a barred arch stands in
   the far wall straight ahead. From where you arrive, do the bars read at a
   glance as bars, a way that will open later, and not as decoration on the
   wall?
2. **The door you meet from the wrong side.** A short passage off the yard's
   side ends at the toll house door. Right-click it from the passage side
   first: does the line read as a promise (this door opens from the other
   side) rather than a refusal? Later you draw its bolt from the toll house
   side and take it as the short way back to the yard: does that loop feel
   right?
3. **The stair worth climbing.** A stone stair beside the yard wall climbs to
   the keeper's walk, where a locked hatch sits beside a chest. Does what is in
   the chest make the climb worth it, rather than a dead end you climbed for
   nothing?
4. **The fireplace.** At the stair's foot, in the same wall, stands the yard's
   fireplace: stone cheeks either side, a carved mantel over it, embers glowing
   on the hearth stone and a dark fireback behind them. Before you take the
   key, does it read as a fireplace and nothing else? Taking the key plays a
   short shot of it: the embers go out and the fireback swings away. After the
   shot, does it read at once as the mouth of a road, as wide as the stair
   beside it?
5. **The stair at its foot.** Stand at the stair's foot, before and after the
   key. Does the stair read as whole, three wide, with the fireplace beside it
   rather than in it?

The machine has already confirmed: every shut way's declared way of being
learned holds; the cart road's mouth is never reached before it opens; and
every door, stair and road is as wide along its whole length as it was
declared, before and after the key, with nothing of the fireplace in the
stair's three columns. What you judge is whether it looks and reads right.

## 给主人看的：要看什么

这是演示关卡，展示"每一条被故事封住的路，都说明玩家如何得知它是封着的"。院子里有三条封路，各用一种方式，另有一条长车道是按"正确做法"搭的。以收费跑腿人的身份走一遍即可，不必赶路。

1. **一眼看见的拱门。** 进入院子时，正对面墙上有一道封着铁栏的拱门。从你出生的位置望过去，铁栏是否一眼就能认出是铁栏、是一条"以后会开"的路，而不是墙上的装饰？
2. **从错的一侧碰到的门。** 院子侧面有一条短过道，尽头是收费房的门。先从过道这一侧去右键它：那句提示读起来是一个承诺（"这门以后能从另一边打开"），还是一句拒绝？之后你会从收费房那一侧拉开门闩，把它当作回院子的近路——这一圈走下来是否顺？
3. **值得爬的石阶。** 院墙边有一段石阶通到看守走道，走道上有一扇锁着的活板门，旁边放着一个箱子。箱子里的东西是否让这段石阶"值得爬"，而不是一个让人白跑的死胡同？
4. **壁炉。** 石阶脚下、同一面墙上，是院子里的壁炉：两侧是石砌炉颊，上面是雕花的壁炉台，炉石上烧着发红的余烬，后面是一面黑色的炉背。拿到钥匙之前，它读起来是不是就是一个壁炉，别无其他？拿到钥匙时会有一个短镜头看向它：余烬熄灭，炉背转开。镜头之后，它是否一眼看得出是一条路的入口，而且和旁边的石阶一样宽？
5. **石阶脚下。** 拿钥匙之前和之后，各站到石阶脚下看一次：石阶读起来是不是完整的三格宽，壁炉在它旁边，而不是挡在它里面？

机器已经确认：每条封路声明的得知方式都成立；车道入口在开之前，玩家根本走不到它面前；每扇门、每段楼梯、每条路在整条路上都和声明的一样宽，拿钥匙前后都是，壁炉的任何部分都没有伸进石阶的三列。你要判断的只是"看起来对不对、读起来对不对"。
