# The Muster Loft

> **Requires delve engine 0.45.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.13.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

A hundred years ago the Harrowgate levy mustered in the hall under the hill and
marched out, and none of them came home. They still fall in on the hall floor
at dusk. The reeve who built the hall barred every way out of it so the dead
could not leave: the loft stair, the cellar hatch, the stores stair, the
grating over the cellar ladder. The first three open when the muster falls;
the grating is raised by a lever in the hall.

This year the reeve's office has sent you down to the hall. Put the muster
down, fetch the levy's roll out of the cellar, and lay it in the loft where
the names are kept, so the levy can be stood down at last.

A demo level of ten to twenty minutes, for one to four players. One short
fight.

## Classes

- **Hall Warden** — a sword, a shield, mail and bread. The reeve's office keeps
  a warden for the hall under the hill, and this year it is you.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → `localhost:25565`. Accept the
resource pack when the server offers it.

## For the owner: what to look for

This is the second round of this level. Three of the four things the first
round found are changed:

- The cellar hatch and the stores stair's hatch were a lattice of iron bars
  that a body fell through. Both are now shut iron trapdoors lying in the
  floor. The machine has stood a body on every point of each, before any beat,
  and seen it stay on top.
- The single iron bar over the ladder's hole is now an iron trapdoor lying on
  the hall floor over it.
- The grindstone that stood in for a winch is gone. A floor lever by the south
  wall, three blocks behind the grating, raises it.

The fourth: the last objective completes the moment you arrive in the loft,
and its title now says so ("Climb to the loft with the roll"); the engine
refuses a title that names an act its completion does not perform.

The loft stair's head is unchanged: iron bars across the passage at its top.
Walk it once as a Hall Warden.

1. **The hatches hold you.** Before the fight the journal walks you to each
   shut way. At the hatch in the hall floor near the foot of the loft stair, and
   at the hatch over the stairwell in the stores, walk out onto the trapdoors
   and stand there. You should stay on top. Do they read as shut hatches the
   story will open, rather than as floor?
2. **The ladder's cover.** The grating in the hall floor between the hatch and
   the stores door is now an iron trapdoor over the ladder's hole. Does it read
   as a cover over a way down? Later, from the cellar, climb the ladder beside
   the timber post: it should stop at the top rung with the cover over your
   head. Does that read as a way shut from above, rather than as a bug?
3. **The fight.** Once all four are tried, the dead levy falls in on the hall
   floor. Put it down. The loft stair's bars and both hatches open the moment
   the last body falls; the ladder's cover stays shut.
4. **The lever.** Drop through the open hatch into the cellar, take the roll
   from the chest in its corner, and try the ladder. Then come back the long
   way: up the stairwell into the stores and through the stores door into the
   hall. A lever now stands on the floor behind the cover, by the south wall.
   Pull it. Does the cover come up, and does the lever read as the thing that
   raises it? The lever is placed when you try the ladder from below; it is not
   there before.
5. **Then the loft.** Climb the loft stair and step into the loft. The delve
   ends there, on arrival (the open finding above).
