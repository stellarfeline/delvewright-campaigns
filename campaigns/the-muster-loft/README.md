# The Muster Loft

> **Requires delve engine 0.45.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.13.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

A hundred years ago the Harrowgate levy mustered in the hall under the hill and
marched out, and none of them came home. They still fall in on the hall floor
at dusk. The reeve who built the hall barred every way out of it so the dead
could not leave: the loft stair, the cellar hatch, the stores stair, the
grating over the cellar ladder. The bars lift when the muster falls.

This year the reeve's office has sent you down to the hall. Put the muster
down, fetch the levy's roll out of the cellar, and lay it in the loft where
the names are kept, so the levy can be stood down at last.

A demo level of ten to twenty minutes, for one to four players. One short
fight.

## Classes

- **Hall Warden** — a sword, a shield, mail and bread. The reeve's office keeps
  a warden for the hall under the hill, and this year it is you.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → `localhost:25565`. Accept the
resource pack when the server offers it.

## For the owner: what to look for

This level is where a gated connection built shut (spec-0107) is looked at in
the game. Every way out of the hall stands barred in blocks from the moment
the world loads, and each bar comes up at the beat that opens it. The machine
has proved that all four bars stand at world load, that no body gets round the
three the story keeps shut (the loft stair, the shaft, the stores stair) by
walking, falling, jumping or swimming, and that the delve can be finished. What
it cannot say is how the bars **read**. Walk it once as a Hall Warden.

1. **Each bar reads as a way the story will open.** Before the fight, the
   journal walks you to each shut way in turn:
   - climb the stone stair up the hall's back wall: at its head, bars fill the
     passage into the loft;
   - the hatch in the hall floor near the foot of that stair: a patch of bars
     over a shaft into the dark;
   - the grating in the hall floor between the hatch and the stores door, with
     a grindstone beside it: one cell of bars with a ladder's top rung under it;
   - through the open door into the stores: a hatch of bars over a stairwell.

   Does each one read as a door that is shut for now, rather than as a wall or
   as decoration?
2. **Three open at the fight's end, one does not.** The dead levy falls in on
   the hall floor once you have tried all four. Put it down. The loft stair's
   bars, the shaft's hatch and the stores stair's hatch should all come up at
   the moment the last body falls; the grating should stay. Do the three read as
   opening at that beat, and does the grating staying read as deliberate?
3. **The ladder's top rung under the grating is a stop.** Drop through the
   open hatch into the cellar and take the roll from the chest in its corner.
   Then climb the ladder beside the timber post. It should take you to its top
   rung and no further, with the grating over your head. Does that feel like
   the stop it is — a way up that is shut from above — or like a bug?
4. **The long way round, and the grating opened from above.** With the ladder
   shut, the way out is up the stairwell into the stores and back through the
   stores door into the hall. Draw the grating at the grindstone beside it.
   Does the grating come up at that beat, and does the ladder now read as the
   short way into the cellar?
5. **Then the loft.** Climb the loft stair, now open, and step into the loft to
   lay the roll down. That ends the delve.
