## How the series was drawn

View 1 is `reference/north-approach`, drawn from its prompt alone and confirmed for style; every other view was drawn from its own prompt anchored on view 1's interaction, under one style contract held constant. The prompts of record are in `design/prompts/`, and each image's sidecar carries the prompt, the style note, the resolved frame and the anchor.

## Where the images and the design differ

- `reference/north-approach` draws the gatehouse's north-east tower square. The design builds it as a stepped many-sided round tower: the grammar has no smooth curve.
- `concept/courtyard-far` adds an arcade along the west side of the courtyard. The design has curtain wall and a postern there.
- `concept/great-hall-far` puts livestock and figures in the courtyard. The courtyard carries a cart and barrels.
- `concept/lords-hall-far` was drawn twice: the first return carried a caption plaque, which the style contract forbids. The second is the approved one. Its upper floor shows modern-looking seating; the design has hangings, a round table and window seats.
