SCENE 5, FAR VIEW — THE TOP OF THE GATEHOUSE, CUT OPEN.

A three-quarter aerial view from the north-east, level with the upper gatehouse, as an architectural cutaway: the gatehouse's roof and its upper east wall are removed so its top two floors show. The duchess's hall with its fireplace and the small oratory alcove in its south wall; beside it, inside the stepped round tower, a bedchamber with a curtained bed; below, the top of the lord's hall. Above, the crenellated parapet walk and the gabled garret. The great hall roof and the kitchen tower beyond, and the valley below.
