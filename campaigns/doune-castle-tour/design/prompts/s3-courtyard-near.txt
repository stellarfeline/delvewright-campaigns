SCENE 3, NEAR VIEW — THE COURTYARD, AT THE WELL.

Eye height in the cobbled courtyard, standing south of the well and looking north at the courtyard face of the north range. In the foreground a round stone well with a timber winding frame and a bucket. Beyond it the whole courtyard front of the north range: on the right the gatehouse with the dark arch of the gate passage at its foot and a straight external stone stair climbing its face to a first-floor doorway; in the middle the great hall's courtyard wall with tall pointed traceried windows high up and a second external stair to its west door; on the left the kitchen tower with its small stair turret and a third stair. The steep hall roof and both towers' parapets against the blue sky. Grass between the cobbles near the walls, a cart and barrels by the kitchen door. A Minecraft villager — the guide — stands beside the well.
