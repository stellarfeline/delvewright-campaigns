SCENE 9, NEAR VIEW — ON THE WALL-WALK.

Eye height on the wall-walk along the top of the south curtain wall, 16 blocks above the courtyard, looking east along it. On the right, the crenellated outer parapet with its regular gaps, and through them the green valley, a river and wooded hills stretching to the horizon. On the left, the drop into the cobbled courtyard with its well far below. Ahead, the wall-walk runs to an open stepped corner round and turns north along the east curtain, a small corbelled bartizan projecting halfway along. Beyond, the north range: the tall gatehouse with its parapet and garret roof, and the steep great hall roof. A Minecraft villager — the guide — stands at a gap in the parapet, looking out.
