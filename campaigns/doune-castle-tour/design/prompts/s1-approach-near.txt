SCENE 1, NEAR VIEW — ON THE BRIDGE BEFORE THE GATE.

The player stands at eye height on the dark oak timber bridge over the last of the three dry ditches, about 12 blocks from the gatehouse, looking up and south at its north front. The gatehouse fills most of the frame and rises out of the top of it: sheer dark coursed stone, lighter dressed quoins, arrow loops, a projecting machicolation box high above the gate. The tall arched gate opening is straight ahead, its iron portcullis raised in the arch, the vaulted passage behind it dark with a bright glimpse of the sunlit courtyard at its far end. The stepped round tower bulges out at the left corner. The ditch below the bridge rails is a steep grassy cut. A Minecraft villager — the guide — stands on the bridge a few blocks ahead, facing the viewer, waiting.
