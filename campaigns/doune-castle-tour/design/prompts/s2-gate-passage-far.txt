SCENE 2, FAR VIEW — THE GATEHOUSE CUT OPEN.

A three-quarter aerial view from the north-east, high above the approach, of the whole gatehouse as an architectural cutaway: its east wall and its roof are removed so the inside shows, floor by floor, while the great hall range and the kitchen tower stand whole behind it to the right. On the ground floor, the long vaulted gate passage runs straight through from the arched north gate to the courtyard, with the guardroom beside it and the pit-prison hatch in its floor. Above it, the first floor is the lord's hall with its big fireplace; above that, the duchess's hall; at the top, the parapet and the gabled garret. The stepped round tower at the corner shows its stacked chambers. The courtyard and the curtain wall are visible beyond.
