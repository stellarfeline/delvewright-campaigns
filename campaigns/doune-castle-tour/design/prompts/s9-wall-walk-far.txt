SCENE 9, FAR VIEW — THE WHOLE CASTLE FROM THE SOUTH.

A three-quarter aerial view from high above the valley floor south of the castle, looking north. The whole castle in its setting: the south curtain wall nearest, its crenellated wall-walk running the full length and turning up the east side, with open stepped corner rounds and small bartizans; inside it the courtyard and well; behind, the north range — the tall gatehouse on the right, the long great hall with its steep roof, the kitchen tower on the left. Around it, the green valley with a river bending past, and the rim of wooded hills all round. The wall-walk circuit is the thing this picture shows.
