SUBJECT — Doune Castle, a late-fourteenth-century Scottish courtyard castle, built in Minecraft blocks at one and a half blocks to the metre. The same castle as the reference image.

PLAN. The NORTH RANGE holds, from east to west: the GATEHOUSE, the Lord's Tower (27 by 20 blocks, 40 blocks tall, a stepped round tower on its north-east corner; the vaulted gate passage runs through its ground floor from the north front into the courtyard; the lord's hall is on its first floor and the duchess's hall on its second); then the GREAT HALL (a long range 30 blocks long, the hall itself on the first floor over three barrel-vaulted cellars, a steep pitched roof, its high table on a dais at the east end against the gatehouse); then the KITCHEN TOWER at the west end (the vaulted kitchen at hall level, the royal apartments stacked above it, a small stair turret on its courtyard corner). A small servery with two arched serving hatches sits between the hall's west end and the kitchen. South of the north range lies an open cobbled COURTYARD with a round stone well at its centre, and three external stone stairs climb from it: one to the lord's hall door in the gatehouse, one to the great hall's west door, one to the kitchen tower. A crenellated CURTAIN WALL 16 blocks high closes the south and east sides of the courtyard, with open stepped corner rounds, small corbelled bartizans at mid-points, and a continuous wall-walk along its top. The castle stands in a green valley ringed by wooded hills.
