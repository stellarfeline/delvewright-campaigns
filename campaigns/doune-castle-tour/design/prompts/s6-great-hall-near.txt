SCENE 6, NEAR VIEW — THE GREAT HALL.

Eye height inside the great hall, standing at the west (screens) end and looking east down its whole length, 30 blocks long, 12 wide, open to a steep timber roof on dark oak trusses 16 blocks up. In the middle of the floor an open hearth of stone with a fire, its smoke rising to a louvre in the roof ridge. Two long trestle tables with benches run down the sides. At the far end, a raised stone dais with the high table across the hall under a cloth canopy, a great chair at its centre, and a door behind it into the lord's hall. Tall pointed windows with glass panes along the right-hand (courtyard) wall pour sunlight in; a hanging iron chandelier with candles; a cupboard of silver plate; banners and hangings. A Minecraft villager — the guide — stands on the dais by the high table.
