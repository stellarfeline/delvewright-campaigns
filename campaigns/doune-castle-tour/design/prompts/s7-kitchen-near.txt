SCENE 7, NEAR VIEW — THE KITCHEN.

Eye height inside the vaulted kitchen of the kitchen tower. Filling the far wall, an enormous stone fireplace about 8 blocks wide under a heavy stone arch, a fire burning on its hearth, an iron spit across it and a cauldron hanging from a swinging crane. Beside it, the domed mouth of a stone bread oven. A heavy oak work table in the middle with bowls and a loaf; barrels, sacks and a stack of logs along the walls; hams and game hanging from hooks in the vault; a stone drain channel in the floor. On the left wall, the backs of two arched serving hatches open through into the servery, where the food went out to the hall. A Minecraft villager — the guide — stands at the fireplace.
