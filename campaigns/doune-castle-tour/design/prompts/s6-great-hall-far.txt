SCENE 6, FAR VIEW — THE HALL RANGE CUT OPEN.

A three-quarter aerial view from the south, above the courtyard, of the great hall range as an architectural cutaway: its roof and its courtyard wall are removed along its whole length. The first-floor hall shows end to end — the dais and high table at the east end against the gatehouse, the central hearth, the trestle tables, the screen at the west end with the servery and its two serving hatches beyond — and below the hall floor, the three barrel-vaulted cellars with casks. The gatehouse stands whole on the right and the kitchen tower whole on the left; the external stair climbs from the courtyard to the hall's west door.
