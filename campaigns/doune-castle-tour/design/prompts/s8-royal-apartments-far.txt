SCENE 8, FAR VIEW — THE KITCHEN TOWER FROM THE WEST.

A three-quarter aerial view from the north-west, level with the upper floors of the kitchen tower, showing the whole west end of the castle: the kitchen tower standing tall at the corner, its upper floors — the royal apartments — shown in cutaway with the west wall removed: the audience chamber with its chair of state and the bedchamber beside it, above the kitchen with its fireplace glimpsed at the bottom. The crenellated parapet and garret on top. The west curtain wall with a small postern gate under a machicolated turret, and the great hall range and the tall gatehouse beyond, to the left.
