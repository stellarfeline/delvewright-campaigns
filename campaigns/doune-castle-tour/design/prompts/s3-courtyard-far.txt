SCENE 3, FAR VIEW — THE COURTYARD FROM ABOVE THE SOUTH-EAST CORNER.

A high three-quarter aerial view from above the castle's south-east corner, looking north-west down into the courtyard over the curtain wall. The crenellated curtain wall with its wall-walk runs along the bottom and right of the frame, with an open stepped corner round in the near corner. Inside it, the open cobbled courtyard with the round well at its centre and the three external stairs against the north range. The north range fills the top of the frame: the tall gatehouse on the right, the long great hall with its steep roof in the middle, the kitchen tower on the left. Beyond the castle, the green valley and the approach road with its three ditches running away north toward wooded hills.
