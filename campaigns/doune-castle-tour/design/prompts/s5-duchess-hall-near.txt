SCENE 5, NEAR VIEW — THE DUCHESS'S HALL AND ITS ORATORY.

Eye height inside the duchess's hall on the second floor of the gatehouse, a smaller, warmer room than the hall below: a timber ceiling on beams, a stone fireplace with a small fire, woven hangings on the walls, a round table with cushioned stools, window seats in deep embrasures with glass panes. In the south wall straight ahead, a small arched oratory alcove: a stone altar with a cloth and two candles, a little stone basin set into the wall beside it, and a shelf niche. Through an open doorway on the left, a glimpse of a bedchamber with a curtained bed in the round tower. A Minecraft villager — the guide — stands at the oratory arch.
