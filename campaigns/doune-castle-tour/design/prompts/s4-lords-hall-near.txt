SCENE 4, NEAR VIEW — THE LORD'S HALL.

Eye height inside the lord's hall on the first floor of the gatehouse, a stone-vaulted room about 24 by 15 blocks. Straight ahead, a great double fireplace of dressed stone with a carved overmantel and a lit fire. A long dark-oak table down the middle with benches, and at its head a high-backed great chair. On the right, a carved dark-oak screen across one end of the room with a timber gallery over it. Deep window embrasures with stone seats, one window looking north over the gate. Red and blue banners on the walls, a sideboard with silver plate, candles and lanterns, rush matting on the stone flags. A Minecraft villager — the guide — stands by the fireplace.
