SCENE 2, NEAR VIEW — INSIDE THE GATE PASSAGE.

Eye height, standing inside the vaulted gate passage that runs 21 blocks through the ground floor of the gatehouse, looking south toward the courtyard. A stone-brick barrel vault overhead with three square murder holes in it; the iron bars of the raised portcullis hang in their slot just behind the viewer; a heavy iron lattice gate (a yett) is folded back against the left wall. Arrow loops and a low doorway on the right lead into a guardroom with a bench and a weapon rack. Lanterns on iron brackets. The far end of the passage is a bright arch full of sunlit courtyard cobbles and the base of the well. A Minecraft villager — the guide — stands near the far end, beside a hatch in the floor with iron bars over it: the pit prison.
