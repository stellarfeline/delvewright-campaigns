SCENE 8, NEAR VIEW — THE ROYAL APARTMENTS.

Eye height inside the audience chamber of the royal apartments, high in the kitchen tower. A handsome room with a beamed timber ceiling and panelled walls of dark oak: at the far end a canopied chair of state on a low step under a cloth of estate; a stone fireplace with a fire; a carpet on the floor; tall glass windows looking out over the valley. Through an open door on the right, a bedchamber with a great curtained bed, a chest and a small fireplace. Candles, a writing table, a tapestry. A Minecraft villager — the guide — stands beside the chair of state.
