SCENE 7, FAR VIEW — THE KITCHEN TOWER CUT OPEN.

A three-quarter aerial view from the south-west, above the courtyard's west side, of the kitchen tower as an architectural cutaway: its roof and its south and west walls are removed so its floors show stacked. At hall level, the vaulted kitchen with its enormous fireplace and bread oven, and the small servery between it and the great hall with the two serving hatches; below it a cellar; above it, the royal apartments — an audience chamber and bedchambers; at the top the parapet and garret. The small stair turret climbs the tower's courtyard corner. The great hall range runs away to the right toward the gatehouse.
