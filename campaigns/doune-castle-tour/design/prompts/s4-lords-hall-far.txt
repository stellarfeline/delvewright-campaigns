SCENE 4, FAR VIEW — WHERE THE LORD'S HALL SITS.

A three-quarter aerial view from the south-east, above the courtyard, of the gatehouse as an architectural cutaway: its south wall above the ground floor and its roof are removed so the first floor shows — the lord's hall with its double fireplace, long table and screen — sitting directly on top of the gate passage, and the duchess's hall above it. The external stone stair climbs from the courtyard to the lord's hall door. To the left, the great hall range stands whole, its east end touching the gatehouse where a door joins the two halls.
