# The Stripped Town — design record

A demo level for declared containers: every container in the built world is
loot, a device or scenery, and the scenery is announced once, at arrival.

## The place

Saltlow, a herring town on a flat shore, emptied for the winter. One street
runs west to east: the cart road comes in at the west end, the stone quay
closes the east end, and Ansel's ferry waits there.

| place | side | what stands in it | containers |
|---|---|---|---|
| the road | west end | two fence posts, the cart track | none |
| the street | — | packed earth and cobbles, five wide, lanterns on posts | none |
| the net-maker's | north, first | bare hooks, a bench, a bed frame | one chest, one barrel — scenery |
| the chandler's | south, first | an empty counter, shelves | one barrel, one chest — scenery |
| the smokehouse | south, second | blackened brick, racks, the smoker | the smoker — loot: a salmon not yet smoked, charcoal, and a smoked cod in the result slot |
| Marra's house | north, last | a bed, a table, a lantern | one chest at the foot of the bed — the parcel (`collect`) |
| the quay | east end | stone, bollards, the ferry's mooring | none |

Two places hold scenery, two each (the engine's cap); the place holding the
parcel holds nothing else; the smoker's place holds nothing else.

## Beats

1. **Arrival.** The class is chosen; the arrival line (the decoration notice)
   tells the party the town took everything that would carry, so its barrels
   and chests stand empty, and only what was left on purpose remains. The
   party stands on the cart road with the whole street ahead.
2. **Meet Ansel** at the quay. He asks them to fetch Marra's parcel from her
   house, the last on the north side, from the chest at the foot of her bed.
3. **The street.** Four open doors on the way. Two houses are stripped; the
   smokehouse is not on the errand, and its smoker holds a smoked fish.
4. **Fetch the parcel** from Marra's chest.
5. **Hand it over** to Ansel at the quay. He takes the party aboard; the
   outro plays.

No branch, no fight, no death. One player is enough; more walk together.
