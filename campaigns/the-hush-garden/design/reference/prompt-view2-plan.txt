The same Hush Garden seen straight down from high above, a true top-down plan with no perspective tilt, the maze square and centred. The hedge-topped walls draw the lanes as a clear maze pattern with loops; the four quarters' floor colours divide it; the round watchtower roof is the dot in the centre court; the small sundial court in the north-west quarter, the dark sculk-ringed well in the south-west corner, four bells at the quarters' middles, and the gate tower on the middle of the east edge opening onto the meadow.
