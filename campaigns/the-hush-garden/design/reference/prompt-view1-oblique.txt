A high three-quarter aerial view of the whole Hush Garden from the south-west, late afternoon. A square hedge maze about fifty blocks across fills the middle of a flat lawn. Its lanes are two blocks wide between one-block walls, winding, with loops and a few dead ends, paved in the four quarter colours. In the exact centre a small square court holds a slender round stone watchtower, about twenty-four blocks tall, with an open lookout deck and a little copper-green roof at the top, and a ladder inside. On the east edge of the maze stands a taller square gate tower of pale stone with a big closed wooden gate at its foot facing out onto a meadow of flowers and a few round trees; its flat top is level with the watchtower deck. In the north-west quarter is a small round court with a stone sundial; in the south-west corner a dark round sunken well of cracked deepslate with sculk creeping from it, the only sombre spot. Four small bronze bells hang on posts at the middles of the four quarters. A low garden wall of brick runs round the lawn.
