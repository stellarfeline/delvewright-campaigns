The same Hush Garden at eye height inside one of its lanes in the mint-green south-west quarter: a two-block-wide paved lane between mossy stone-brick walls four blocks high capped with clipped hedge, so nothing beyond the walls is visible except the sky and, rising over the wall tops, the slender watchtower with its copper-green roof. The lane turns left ahead; a small bronze bell hangs on a post at the next junction. Warm late sun on the wall tops, cool shade on the floor.
