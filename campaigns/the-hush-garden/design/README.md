# The Hush Garden — the approved design

**Author from the image, judge against it, present every choice beside it.**

These pictures are the approved design: style, mood and what each place is. They are never dimensions; the numbers are `../geometry-brief.json` and `../DESIGN.md`, and the maze's pattern is its generator's.

All are drawn on `gemini-native` (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) under one style contract (`reference/style.txt`, sent as `--style-note`); view 1 from its prompt alone, every other picture chained on view 1's interaction (`request.chain_from` in its sidecar). Every picture is late afternoon (`{"daytime":10500}`) and clear.
