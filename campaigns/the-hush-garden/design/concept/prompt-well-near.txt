Scene: the old well in the green quarter's far corner, close up at eye height. A low ring of cracked deepslate bricks around a black shaft, dark sculk spreading over the mint-paved floor around it with faint teal glints, and the warden — the game's tall dark blue-grey creature with glowing teal chest and horn-like ears — halfway dug up out of the ground beside the well, clawing out of the soil, dirt flying. The hedge walls close around it. The light is still warm afternoon, the only sombre spot in the garden.
