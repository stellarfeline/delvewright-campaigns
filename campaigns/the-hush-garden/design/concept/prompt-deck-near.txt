Scene: on the watchtower's lookout deck at eye height, looking east. A small railed deck of spruce planks under a little copper-green roof on posts. In the foreground a stone lectern with four stone push-buttons in a row, each under a small painted sign of a bell in one quarter's colour: rose, butter-yellow, pale blue, mint. Beyond the rail, far below, the maze's hedge-capped walls and coloured lanes laid out like a map. On the far east edge of the maze, far away, the pale-stone gate tower, its flat top level with this deck, and between the deck and that tower nothing but open air above the maze. A flower meadow beyond, low golden sun.
