Scene: the heart of the Hush Garden, at eye height. A small square court paved in pale stone in the middle of the maze, ringed by the four-block hedge-capped walls, lane mouths opening in each side. In the middle the foot of the slender round watchtower, a small arched door in its base with a ladder visible inside. Beside it a wooden garden bench with a folded paper note weighted down by a little flowerpot. Two lanterns on posts. Late-afternoon sun on the tower, the court half in shade.
