View 3, the same town as the reference image, Kelderby as a straight-down plan from directly overhead, north at the top of the frame, in the same hand and the same late-afternoon light with shadows falling to the east. At the top, the green mountain with the castle on its summit: a square grey courtyard castle with a gatehouse tower, a hall range and a kitchen tower along its north side, a cobbled courtyard and a curtain wall. A cobbled road zig-zags down the mountain from the castle to a rectangular cobbled square at the mountain's foot, near the upper middle of the frame. On the square's east side the cross-shaped roof of a large cathedral with its long nave running east and a walled churchyard to its south; on the square's west side the L-shaped roof of a coaching inn around its yard. From the square's south side one broad straight main street runs due south down the middle of the frame to a long straight stone quay along the harbour basin at the bottom; tiled roofs of houses and yards line both sides of the street, and three narrow side lanes leave it east and west. The lowest lane on the east runs out to a cliff path that follows the spine of a rocky headland south, on the east side of the harbour basin, to a white round lighthouse with a cottage in a walled yard at its southern tip. Deep blue sea at the bottom and right; the coast curving away on the left.
