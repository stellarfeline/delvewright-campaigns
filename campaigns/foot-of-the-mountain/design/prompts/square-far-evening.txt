SUBJECT - Kelderby, the same town as the reference image, in the same hand. A cobbled square at the top of the town at the mountain's foot, a large Gothic cathedral on its east side and a timber-framed coaching inn, The Bell, on its west side; one broad main street stepping south down the slope in short flights of shallow steps to a long stone quay on the harbour; side lanes off it; a rocky headland east of the harbour with a cliff path to a tall white lighthouse; a grey Scottish courtyard castle on the mountain's summit, about eighty blocks above the square, reached by a long cobbled road zig-zagging up the mountain's flank. The town is dressed for its yearly Founders' Play: bunting and painted banners.

SCENE 7, FAR VIEW - THE SQUARE FROM THE CATHEDRAL STEPS IN THE LAST HOUR BEFORE SUNSET. Standing on the cathedral's west steps looking west and south over the crowded square: the wooden stage with its curtain open and a performance underway, benches full of townsfolk, the timber-framed inn with lit windows, lanterns and bunting; beyond, the town's roofs stepping down the main street to the harbour, and the sea with the low evening sun still well above the horizon. Late golden evening, the sky clear and still blue overhead, deep golden light and long shadows, the first lanterns lit. Every sign and banner carries a painted picture only, never any lettering.
