View 2, the same town as the reference image, Kelderby seen from the castle gate on the mountain's summit, looking south and down over the whole town to the sea, in late afternoon light. The same town, the same hand. In the foreground the grey stone gatehouse of the castle and the top of the cobbled castle road, which zig-zags down the green mountain flank in long level stretches joined by short stair-ramps, about eighty blocks down to the cobbled town square at the mountain's foot. On the square: the large Gothic cathedral on its left (east) side, its long nave and apse running away to the left, its tall bell tower at the corner nearest the square; the timber-framed coaching inn on its right (west) side; a well and two trees in the middle. From the square's far side the one broad main street runs straight down the slope away from the viewer, southward, in short flights of shallow steps, bunting across it, houses on both sides with their roofs stepping down, three narrow side lanes leaving it, to the long stone quay and harbour basin at the bottom. On the left (east) of the harbour, a rocky green headland runs out to sea with a narrow cliff path along its spine to the tall white lighthouse at its tip. Beyond everything, the wide deep blue sea to the horizon, the sun low on the right (west).
