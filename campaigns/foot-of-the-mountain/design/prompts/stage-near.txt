SUBJECT - Kelderby, the same town as the reference image, in the same hand. A cobbled square at the top of the town at the mountain's foot, a large Gothic cathedral on its east side and a timber-framed coaching inn, The Bell, on its west side; one broad main street stepping south down the slope in short flights of shallow steps to a long stone quay on the harbour; side lanes off it; a rocky headland east of the harbour with a cliff path to a tall white lighthouse; a grey Scottish courtyard castle on the mountain's summit, about eighty blocks above the square, reached by a long cobbled road zig-zagging up the mountain's flank. The town is dressed for its yearly Founders' Play: bunting and painted banners.

SCENE 7, NEAR VIEW - THE STAGE ON THE SQUARE IN THE LAST HOUR BEFORE SUNSET. Eye height in front of a wooden stage built on the cobbled square, the curtain open: on the stage a performer in a great green-and-gold dragon costume, a girl in a fisher's smock playing the Founder holding a round loaf, painted scenery of a boat and a mountain; townsfolk in rows of benches in the foreground, lanterns lit along the stage's front edge; the cathedral's west front lit deep gold by the low sun behind the stage. Late golden evening: the sun low in the west, still well above the sea, the sky clear and still blue overhead, deep golden light and long shadows. Every sign and banner carries a painted picture only, never any lettering.
