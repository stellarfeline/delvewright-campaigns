SUBJECT - Kelderby, the same town as the reference image, in the same hand. A cobbled square at the top of the town at the mountain's foot, a large Gothic cathedral on its east side and a timber-framed coaching inn, The Bell, on its west side; one broad main street stepping south down the slope in short flights of shallow steps to a long stone quay on the harbour; side lanes off it; a rocky headland east of the harbour with a cliff path to a tall white lighthouse; a grey Scottish courtyard castle on the mountain's summit, about eighty blocks above the square, reached by a long cobbled road zig-zagging up the mountain's flank. The town is dressed for its yearly Founders' Play: bunting and painted banners.

Lady Agathe, an elderly tall lady with silver hair up and a green velvet coat, stands beside a homemade theatre costume of a dragon hung on a wooden stand: an obviously stitched green-and-gold cloth body over a wicker frame, a big friendly papier-mache dragon head with button eyes, an empty space inside it for a wearer, and a long stuffed cloth tail curling across the floor; in the tail's curl a black jackdaw sits on a messy twig nest. An old steward, Bernt, in castle grey and black, waits by the door. Floating near the high roof, a Minecraft happy ghast: a big soft white CUBE-shaped creature with a small smiling square face and short dangling tentacles beneath it, wearing a little harness. Warm, funny, gentle. Late afternoon, clear sky, golden sun low in the west.
