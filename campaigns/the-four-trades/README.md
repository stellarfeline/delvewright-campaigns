# The Four Trades

> **Requires delve engine 0.66.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.15.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

Craft Lane runs off the market road. A smith, a weaver, a baker and a
cartographer keep shop along it, and the lane ends at the old well.

A test level of five minutes or so, for one to four players. No fight. The
well is the only place you must reach; look in at any shop on the way.

## Classes

- **Traveller** — a walking staff and bread.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → the address the host gives you.
Accept the resource pack when the server offers it.

---

## For the owner: what to look for

This test level shows one thing: a building reads as its trade from its
threshold. Four two-room shops stand on the lane's north side, each a front
shop a customer enters and a workroom behind a wall with a window and a door,
where the trade is worked. Each place's design row carries two human-built
references, and the build draws a frame from every door's sill.

Stand in each doorway and, before you read anything, name the trade:

1. **The smithy** — the forge, the anvil and the quench behind the window;
   finished ironwork behind the counter.
2. **The weaver's** — the shape that was judged bad, built right: looms with
   their seats in the workroom, bolts of cloth on the shelves and cloth on the
   counter in the shop.
3. **The bakery** — the brick oven burning behind the window, flour sacks and
   wheat behind the counter, smoke from the chimney.
4. **The cartographer's** — charts on the wall and a map table in the
   reception room, drawing tables and shelves of surveys in the map room.

Then step in: is the room a customer enters a different room from the one the
work is done in?

The machine has already confirmed: every trade stands on fourteen by fourteen
of play space, its width bound to that figure in the plan; every door — eight, the four front doors and
the four between the rooms — is framed by wall and head; a threshold frame is
drawn from each of the eight doorways and the well-yard's four sides; the bot
walked the lane to the well.

## 给主人看的：要看什么

这个测试关卡只展示一件事：一座建筑站在门槛上就能看出是做哪一行的。小巷北边立着四家两间屋的铺子，每家都有一间顾客走进来的前铺，和一堵带窗带门的墙后面的工坊，手艺就在工坊里做。每个地方的设计行里都带着两条真人建筑的参考，构建会从每扇门的门槛上画一张画面。

站在每个门口，先别读任何说明，说出这是哪一行：

1. **铁匠铺**——窗后面是炉子、铁砧和淬火的水；柜台后面是打好的铁器。
2. **织布坊**——就是之前被判为不好的那种，这次按对的方式建：工坊里是织布机，每台前面有座位；前铺的架子上是一匹匹布，柜台上摊着布。
3. **面包房**——窗后面砖炉烧着火，柜台后面是面粉袋和麦子，烟囱冒着烟。
4. **地图师的铺子**——接待室墙上挂着图，有一张摆地图的桌子；地图室里是绘图桌和一架架测绘册。

然后走进去：顾客走进的那间屋，和干活的那间屋，是不是两间不同的屋子？

机器已经确认：每家铺子都有十四乘十四的活动空间，宽度在场地规划里按这个数绑定；每扇门——八扇，四扇前门和四扇两间屋之间的门——都有墙和门楣框住；八个门口和井院的四面各画了一张门槛画面；机器人沿着小巷走到了井边。
