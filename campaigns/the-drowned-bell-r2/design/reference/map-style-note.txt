Each image in the series is ONE SINGLE FULL-FRAME VIEW of that place, filling the frame edge to edge: never a sheet, never a grid, never divided into panels, never with an inset, a border, a caption or a label. Draw exactly and only the one view the prompt names, and do not repeat any earlier view of the series inside it.
