# The Drowned Bell — reconciliation across the produced zones

Each zone is authored from its own reference image and judged against that image.
This document asks the question no zone's own record can: **taken together, what
did the eight images ask for that the built zones do not contain — and does the
set of omissions mean something the individual omissions do not?**

It is written from the produced zones' own "Open against this piece" lists. It
adjudicates nothing about placement; where each zone sits in the massing belongs
to the whole-map reference, and this document is one of its inputs.

## What each zone dropped

| Zone | The image asked for | The zone contains |
|---|---|---|
| Z1 崖道 | a road projecting from the cliff on a lip, the drop undercut beneath it | a walking surface flush with the rock; the seaward elevation is a flat plane with a seam |
| Z1 崖道 | rusted iron stanchions and a chain rail | no role and no rule — the element that reads the road as *made* rather than found |
| Z2 门楼 | a running channel through the gate passage | still water at the plane's height, or a dry gutter |
| Z4 礼拜堂中庭 | an open, roofless cloister | an enclosed hub — palette and proportion converge, composition deliberately does not |
| Z5 大厅与主楼 | a hearth, tapestries, a far arch | no role and no rule for any of the three; the lower landing is bare floor |
| Z7 钟塔 | a curved bell, a cornice at the tower's head, weathering that varies down the shaft | a stepped silhouette, a flat cap, uniform weathering |
| every zone | — | `dark`, with no light-emitting role in any program |
| every zone | — | no declared spatial contract, so the way-in proof counts cells on a region face |

Two rows are **decisions with stated reasons and are not omissions**: Z2's still
water (a running channel is a claim the physics cannot keep, and a brimming
gutter tells the same story) and Z4's enclosed topology (fixed by design). They
are listed because they change what the place looks like from outside, which is a
site-plan fact whoever draws the whole needs, not because they are defects.

## The omissions are all one omission

Read down the column and the dropped items are not a scatter. Stanchions and a
chain rail. A hearth and tapestries. A cornice. A furnished landing. Light.

**Every one of them is the made-by-hands layer.** What the zones do contain is
mass: rock, wall, floor, stair, the shape of a room. What they consistently do
not contain is the evidence that people built this place and lived in it.

That is invisible zone by zone — each list reads as one or two forgivable items
against a piece that passes every gate — and it is the whole campaign's largest
risk when the parts are assembled. **Eight correctly-massed volumes with no
fittings and no light do not read as a citadel. They read as a quarry.**

The universal `dark` row is the same omission, not a separate one: a lamp is a
fitting. No zone's palette can light it because no zone's palette contains
anything that was *put there* rather than *cut from* the rock.

## Two of them are on the silhouette, which is where it costs most

Z1's seaward elevation is flat where the image has an undercut lip. Z7's tower
head is a flat cap where the image has a cornice. Those are the sea front and the
skyline — the two faces a player reads the place by from outside, and the two the
constitution says carry the recognition that detail cannot.

So the simplification did not land evenly. It landed hardest on the profile.

## What this means for the whole-map work

The composition model adds "connecting pieces and decorative work" on top of the
zone programs. This reconciliation says what that decorative work actually **is**:
not garnish on a finished castle, but the missing layer that separates a citadel
from caves — fittings, ornament, light, and the relief on the two faces that
carry the silhouette.

It is therefore not a step that can be deferred to polish. A whole-map program
whose connective tissue is only walls and seams composes eight quarries into one
larger quarry, and every machine gate stays green while it does.

## What is not settled here

Whether any individual omission is a capability gap or an authoring gap. One is
known to be a capability gap — Z1's lip, where the only lever couples the
projection to the width of the drop. The others have not been triaged, and a
triage that assumes "no rule exists" means "the language cannot" would be the
same error at the other end.
