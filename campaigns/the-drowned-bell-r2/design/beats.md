# The Drowned Bell — beat sheet

Zone by zone in the player's ascent order. **Every beat names the element of its
zone's concept image it is built on.** A beat that cannot point at the image is
not ready and does not appear here.

Zone names are the approved names (`README.md`). Times are the intended share of
a 2–3 hour run. Each zone states the **tide level** it is played at; the sea is
one moving plane and its full design is `tide.md`.

**Scale carries content** (constitution, DEC-0078). Where a beat below implies a
large volume, the volume is listed in *What fills it* at the end of this
document. A zone that encloses air it has no play in is a small zone charging a
long walk, and none of these are that.

## Z0 · 冢泽潮滩 — Barrow Shore · `concept/z0-barrow-shore.jpg` · ~10 min

**Tide: `T-EBB1`, and `T-DEAD` on the optional return.** The night's first low
water. Flat grey mud, fog, the rock somewhere above in it.

| # | beat | image element |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | The player wakes face-down on wet flat with a lead passage-token in hand. First thing visible is a heap of stones a few metres away. | the drystone cairns standing out of the sand |
| 0.2 | The cairns are graves — one per person recovered off the flat, heaped where they were found. They thin out going inland and crowd at the tide-line. Reading three of them is the delve's first optional prose. | the two large cairns foreground-left and the collapsed low wall right |
| 0.3 | **First enemy.** One of the answered pushes up out of the mud beside a cairn: slow, unarmed, walking landward past the player without looking at them. It is the tutorial kill and it is meant to feel like an unkindness. | the black weed heaped over the stones, which is what it is wearing |
| 0.4 | **初见杀 K1 — the tide-stakes.** Three iron stakes stand in a line. Inside the line the flat is walkable; a step outside it and the mud takes the player. The cairns are densest exactly *at* the line, which is the tell. | the three rusted iron posts |
| 0.5 | **Emeric Tallow, the Lampman**, is on the last dry shelf with a shore-lamp lit in full daylight. Living, old, waiting for this specific tide. He explains the three tolls and refuses to go further up than he already is. | the shelving stone and low walling at the right where the flat meets the cliff |
| 0.6 | The way on is the only way on: the flat ends at a wall of cliff and one cut ledge starts up it. | the cliff faces receding into fog at the back and right |
| 0.7 | **The return, at the Dead Ebb.** After `TIDE-4` the flat is bare and lower than living memory. Below the old stake line, on ground nobody has stood on since before the Long Tide, the rest of the answered are standing in the silt, facing the rock. Optional, wordless, and the delve's strongest image that is not the bell. | the shallow water at the left of the frame, which is where they are |

**Shortcut endpoint:** Z0 is the far end of shortcut **S1**. The shortcut's walk
is taken at `T-EBB1`, one beat before the sea closes the flat; it re-opens at
the Dead Ebb onto 0.7, which is somewhere new rather than somewhere revisited.

## Z1 · 崖道 — Cliff Road · `concept/z1-cliff-road.jpg` · ~15 min

**Tide: any — the main ledge is dry at every level.** A cut ledge on a vertical
face, surf a long way down. The first place the delve can kill for a mistake
rather than for a fight.

| # | beat | image element |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | The ledge is single-file. Anything met here is met head-on with no room to circle — the zone teaches spacing before the delve ever teaches damage. | the ledge narrowing to about one body's width |
| 1.2 | A row of iron brackets is driven into the wall at hand height: the anchors of a rope handrail that is long gone. They are the zone's only navigation aid and its only warning. | the line of rusted iron pitons and rings in the rock face |
| 1.3 | **初见杀 K2 — the fallen ledge.** Where the brackets are bent down or missing, the shelf under them has already gone into the sea. The gap is around a blind bend and it is a straight fall. Lesson: read the wall, not the floor. At the flood the sea rises into this one gap and no other — the tide **confirms** the zone's lesson and never contradicts it. | the bent lower bracket and the undercut section of shelf |
| 1.4 | **A shover.** One of the answered on the ledge that grapples rather than strikes, and puts the player over the edge. It is the reason the lesson of 1.3 has to be learned before this. | the surf and rock teeth directly below the path |
| 1.5 | The road goes **through** the rock: a rope store cut into the face, where the priory kept the handrail it stopped replacing. The ledge enters at one mouth and leaves at the next, so **the Z1 rope** — coiled, salt-hard, and the rope the bell will need — cannot be walked past. It is one of the delve's two carried key items and the route is built around not missing it. | the two dark cave mouths in the cliff wall |
| 1.6 | The road does not reach the gate. It reaches a **breach** — a sea-broken hole in the gatehouse's outer wall, the priory's own wall failing. The player enters Halgrave through the back of the gate passage, which is the whole of the next zone's design. | the path bending out of sight into the rock at upper right |

## Z2 · 门楼 — Gatehouse · `concept/z2-gatehouse.jpg` · ~20 min

**Tide: `T-EBB1`, stepping to `T-STAND` at beat 2.6.** The delve's first
architecture, and its first shortcut. The player arrives on the **inside** of a
gate that is shut, and the zone is about opening it outward.

| # | beat | image element |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Entry through the breach puts the player in the gate passage behind a lowered portcullis. Through its bars, the shore they walked in from — visibly, humiliatingly close. | the broken wall opening to sea and sky at the left, and the view out to the cliffs |
| 2.2 | **初见杀 K3 — the murder-holes.** The vault over the passage has a row of round openings. Walking the centre line of the passage draws a killing drop from above. The edges are safe. The lesson is also a map: there is a floor above, and something on it. | the four round openings in the ceiling vault |
| 2.3 | A cut channel runs down the middle of the passage and out under the gate — the priory's drain. It is a wet gutter at the ebbs and brims to its lip at the flood: the rock's outlet has become the rock's inlet, which is the whole of Halgrave's fifty-one years in a detail two blocks wide. It is still water at the plane's height, never a flow. | the water channel cut down the centre of the flagstones |
| 2.4 | **Optional gate G1 — the guardroom.** The porter's embrasure is grated shut, with the crossing-ledger's loose leaves blown out across the sill and floor. The leaves are readable and say nothing; the bound ledger is inside. | the iron grate laid across the embrasure sill and the scattered papers |
| 2.5 | **Optional elite — the Gatewright**, on the gatehouse roof above the murder-holes, still working a winch that has nothing left to lift. Carries the guardroom key. Fully walkable-past: the roof stair is off the critical path and the player can see him before committing. | the vault above the passage, reached from the ruined wall-head at the left |
| 2.6 | **Shortcut S1 — the portcullis**, and **tide step `TIDE-1`.** The winch is on the passage's inner side. Raising it turns the gate into the front door — and as the grate finishes rising, the player is standing at the winch looking down the passage at the shore they woke on, and the sea comes up over it. The shortcut is paid out one beat before it is drowned, deliberately, and the delve closes its own back door in front of the player rather than behind them. | the raised portcullis with its rusted spiked bottom, and the view out through it |
| 2.7 | Emeric moves up to the gate passage once S1 is open and stays there for the rest of the delve. The lamp goes with him. | the passage interior, dry and roofed — the first shelter in the delve |

## Z3 · 下沉外庭 — Drowned Ward · `concept/z3-drowned-ward.jpg` · ~25 min

**Tide: `T-STAND` on the first crossing; `T-FLOOD` after 4.6 closes it;
`T-DEAD` on the return, when its floor is dry.** The outer ward's floor is below
the standing tide, which is why it has been under water for fifty-one years —
it is not a pool, it is the sea, standing in a ruin the priory built when the
sea was lower. The zone is a lesson in geometry: the raised path is safe, the
water is not, and the player chooses when to leave the path.

| # | beat | image element |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | The gate opens onto a ward with a flooded floor and two arcades standing in it. The causeway is a raised stone spine crossing it, above the water, and the route is visible end to end from the start. | the raised causeway crossing the flooded ward toward the tower |
| 3.2 | **Waders.** The answered stand in the flooded ward, slow, many, spread out. They cannot climb the causeway quickly. Every one of them is optional; a player who never steps off the spine fights almost nothing here, and that is the intended reading. | the flooded floor and the arcade piers standing in it |
| 3.3 | The causeway is fouled with heaped weed at two points, forcing the player to slow at exactly the reach of a wader. The zone's difficulty is entirely in its two pinches. | the black weed heaped across the causeway's near section |
| 3.4 | **Optional gate G4 — the wrecks.** Boats lie sunk off the causeway's left. One is Odo's, and in it is the **ferry-tally**: every crossing he made, the last line uncrossed, with the player's name on it. Reaching it means a swim through the water the zone has just taught the player to fear. | the sunken boats in the water at the left |
| 3.5 | The causeway ends at a square tower with a peaked roof and shuttered windows, standing on its own base in the water — a water-gate tower, the ward's checkpoint. Its ground door is **barred from the inside**. | the tower with the metal peaked roof and shuttered windows |
| 3.6 | The route in is over the arcade: the player climbs the ruined arcade top, enters the tower at shutter height, and descends inside. | the intact arcade run at the right, level with the tower's mid-storey |
| 3.7 | **Shortcut S2 — the barred door.** Unbarred from within, and the delve's clearest statement of its own grammar: a door that told you, from the far side, that it did not open from there, and now does. | the tower's ground-level opening at the causeway's end |
| 3.8 | **The ward at the Dead Ebb.** After `TIDE-4` the player comes out of the cistern grate onto a ward with no water in it: a silt plain of weed, sunk boats and fifty-one years of dropped things, the arcades standing over it, the causeway now a wall through a field. The zone the player knows best is the last new zone in the delve, and it costs nothing to build because it is the same zone. | the whole flooded floor, at 2.5 m lower |

## Z4 · 礼拜堂中庭 — Chapel Ward · `concept/z4-chapel-ward.jpg` · ~25 min

**Tide: above every level; the sea never reaches this zone.** Open sky, open
ground, and the first fight the player has room to lose properly.

| # | beat | image element |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | The ward is a cloister with its roof gone: arcade on all four sides, sky above, grass in the joints of the paving. After two enclosed zones the space itself is the event. | the arcades on all sides under an open, broken roofline |
| 4.2 | **初见杀 K4 — the fallen.** Bodies lie among the rubble piles as scenery for the whole of the first crossing. One of them is not scenery. Once learned, it is permanent: this delve's corpses are a question. | the rubble and fallen masonry heaped along the left arcade |
| 4.3 | **The Two Sextons** — the pair who carried the dead in from the boats, still working as a pair, still moving in step. The delve's first fight that punishes fighting one enemy at a time. Critical path; the ward cannot be crossed around them. | the collapsed canopy structure at the back left, which is what they are working under |
| 4.4 | **Sister Ide of the Hours** walks the cloister round with a cracked hand-bell, ringing the hour into a ruin. Non-hostile, lucid, in the middle of the arena — she does not stop for the Sextons and she does not thank the player for them. | the standing plinth at the left of the ward, which is her turning-point |
| 4.5 | **Optional gate G2 — the rite.** Ide will teach the three tolls, in exchange for them being rung properly: her hand-bell, sounded at three marked stations of her round. Completing it opens the **hour-vault** below the chapel. | the three surviving arcade bays she circles between |
| 4.6 | **Shortcut S3 — the banded door**, and **tide step `TIDE-2`.** The one intact door on the rock: oak, iron-banded, a ring handle, barred on the chapel side. Opened, it drops the player straight back into the gatehouse yard — and the view through it as it swings is the drowned ward below going under the flood, on the route they have just made unnecessary. A shortcut that becomes the only route is worth more than a shortcut that saves a walk, and the tide gives that for nothing. | the closed iron-banded double door with the ring handle, and the arcade gap beside it that the ward is visible through |
| 4.7 | Emeric will come this far, once S3 is open, and no further. He sets the lamp on the chapel step. This is where his confession becomes available. | the chapel step under the banded door's arch |

## Z5 · 大厅与主楼 — Hall & Keep · `concept/z5-hall-keep.jpg` · ~25 min

**Tide: the sea has never been in this room, in fifty-one years or in four
hundred.** Indoors, dry, and inhabited by the living arrangement of a dead
household. The only zone where the enemy is a person rather than a consequence,
and the only zone the tide does not touch at all — which is characterisation:
Ancel's household is the part of Halgrave that never got wet.

| # | beat | image element |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | The hall is intact: roof on, floor swept, dust standing in the light. It is the first place in the delve that is not a ruin, and that is worse. | the timber roof trusses and the shafts of light through the slit windows |
| 5.2 | The great hearth is cold, laid, and never lit — kindling under the andirons, fifty-one years old. Ancel's household has been waiting for an order to light it. | the fireplace with cold ash and the two iron fire-dogs |
| 5.3 | The tapestries are the priory's own account of itself: crossings, tolls, a bell, a prior. The last panel is unfinished and the loom is not in the room. | the hanging tapestries on the left wall |
| 5.4 | **Boss 1 — Housecarl Ridd.** Ancel's guard captain, armoured, human, alone, fought in the open hall. The first opponent who blocks, waits, and takes the fight seriously. He is the man who broke the belfry stair, and he will say so. | the hall's clear central floor between the hearth and the far arch |
| 5.5 | **Prior Ancel Wray** sits in the far chamber past the arch, in his chair, dead and entirely lucid. Not a fight, at any point, in any branch. He answers for the two tolls, and does not think he was wrong. | the arched doorway at the right, and the chairs visible in the dim room beyond |
| 5.6 | **Optional gate G3 — the prior's chamber** proper, behind Ancel: Ridd's key opens it. The confession is inside, written and never sent, and the crossing-ledger's matching half. | the second dim chamber beyond the arch |
| 5.7 | Ancel gives up the two facts the critical path needs, because he sees no danger in them — and he is right that they have never been dangerous before: the tongue went down the cistern well, where it has been under water for fifty-one years, and the belfry stair is broken at the first flight. What he does not know is what the tide is doing tonight. | the arch to the keep stair |
| 5.8 | **Tide step `TIDE-3`** fires as the player *leaves*, on the descent stair: the lower flight is running wet, the sea going out ahead of them, heard from a room the sea has never entered. | the sconces along the right wall, lit, leading down |

## Z6 · 深蓄水池 — Cistern Deep · `concept/z6-cistern-deep.jpg` · ~25 min

**Tide: `T-STAND` on arrival, stepping to `T-DEAD` at beat 6.5.** The climb's
last move is a descent. Everything above this point was going up. This zone is
the reason the tide is in the campaign at all.

| # | beat | image element |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | A brick barrel-vault under the rock, standing ankle-deep, lit by one hole. It is the delve's largest interior and it is **ranked**: the vaults march away on piers, so the plan is legible from anywhere and the player always knows which bay they are in. The quiet is the point — their own footsteps are the loudest thing in the delve. | the vaulted brickwork, the pier ranks, and the standing water across the whole floor |
| 6.2 | The daylight shaft falls through a collapse in the vault. It is the zone's only landmark and every route is described from it. | the collapsed hole in the vault and the shaft of light through it |
| 6.3 | **初见杀 K5 — the channel.** The floor is ankle-deep except along the old supply channel, which is a drop, and is invisible except that its surface is darker. The shaft's reflection is the tell: the light lies flat on shallow water and goes black over the channel. The channel's bed is below every tide level including the Dead Ebb, so it holds water in every state and the tell is never withdrawn. | the dark band of water reflecting under the light shaft |
| 6.4 | **Optional elite — the Choir.** Three of the answered stand in a side vault where the echo is best, making the sound of a bell with no bell. They do not move until approached and the player can hear exactly how many there are before committing. | the receding dark vaults at the right |
| 6.5 | **Boss 2 — the Founder.** The man who cast Mercy, was made to strike out its tongue, threw it down this well and came down after it. He is between the player and the well, he is fought standing in the ankle-deep water, and he is comfortable in it. **Tide step `TIDE-4`** fires as he goes down: he falls in water that then leaves from under him, and the well drains. | the well head and its apron, at the deep end of the vault |
| 6.6 | **The tongue.** Copper, the length of a man, lying in silt that has not seen air since the year it was thrown there — the Dead Ebb clears the well's bed by a hand's breadth, for about two hours, once in fifty-one years. The delve's key item is not awarded and not unlocked. It is uncovered by the sea. | the shaft of light, now falling into a well with no water in it |
| 6.7 | **Shortcut S4 — the grille.** An iron grate set in a broken wall, openable only from the cistern side, that comes out where the drowned ward's waterline used to be. Broken outward at the Dead Ebb, it opens onto the ward's bare floor (3.8) and turns the delve's longest walk-back into twenty seconds. | the fallen iron grille half out of the hole in the brick wall at the left |

## Z7 · 钟塔 — Bell Tower · `concept/z7-bell-tower.jpg` · ~25 min

**Tide: above every level; the tide gives this zone the view and the endings,
and nothing else.** From the belfry the whole rock is visible at the lowest
water in fifty-one years — every zone the player has crossed, changed.

| # | beat | image element |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | The approach is a cobbled ramp between the collapsed low buildings of the upper ward, with the tower standing at the head of it — the first time the whole climb is visible as a shape. | the cobbled ramp rising between ruined walls toward the tower |
| 7.2 | **Shortcut S5 — the tower foot.** The small arched door at the base is barred from within and is opened after the belfry, not before. It is the ending's walk-back and the boss's retry path. | the dark arched opening at the tower's foot |
| 7.3 | The broken first flight. The stair is visible through the tower's slit from outside and from inside it simply stops — this is what the household did to the tower, seen from the wrong end. The Z1 rope and the tower's own iron brackets close the gap. | the stair visible through the tall mid-height slit opening |
| 7.4 | The climb up the tower passes the drowned going the same way in the dark, and they do not fight on the stair. This is the delve's clearest statement that the player is one of them. | the narrow slit windows up the tower's height |
| 7.5 | **Final boss — Odo Ferrier**, at the stairhead below the belfry floor: the first of the answered, who has made this climb every tide for fifty-one years and means to be the last hand himself. Beaten, he does not die; he sits down on the stair. | the belfry floor's underside, above the stairhead |
| 7.6 | **The pacifist route.** Carrying the ferry-tally from Z3, the player can show Odo his own last line. He stands aside — his passenger goes first — and follows the player up. | the open belfry, seen from the stairhead |
| 7.7 | **Mercy.** The bell is **built** — a structure of oxidised copper in a timber frame that is part of the building, and the belfry is a room the player walks around it in rather than a shelf it sits on. Its size is the reason the coast can hear it, which is the reason the whole story happened. | the bell in the open belfry, at the size the image gives it against the tower |
| 7.8 | The tongue is hung, the rope is bent on, and the delve's last interaction is a number: three, two, or the frame. Ringing is a haul on a rope at the frame, at the scale of the thing being moved. Whoever the player has brought this far is standing in the belfry when it goes. | the open belfry's four faces, which is what the sound leaves through |

Gated and optional content — the four gates G1–G4 and the three optional elites
(the Gatewright, the Choir, the First Turned) — is approximately 20% of the
run's content, and none of it is on the critical path.

Also on the Dead Ebb, and costing no new zone: the drowned ward's floor (3.8)
and the flat below the stake line (0.7). Both are the delve's existing geometry
at a level nobody has seen, which is the tide paying for itself.

## What fills it

Grandeur is playable content, not enclosed air (DEC-0078). Halgrave is built
grand — it is a fortified priory that bankrupted a coast — and every large
volume below is listed with the play that fills it. **A room that cannot fill
this column is built smaller.**

| zone | the volume | what fills it |
|---|---|---|
| Z0 | a half-mile of open flat | the cairn field (readable, one per recovered body), the tide-stake line as a lethal boundary, the tutorial kill, Emeric's shelf, and at the Dead Ebb the ground below the stake line and what is standing on it |
| Z1 | a cliff face | the ledge itself as a one-body corridor, the bracket line as the zone's only map, the K2 gap, the shover, and the rope store the road passes through |
| Z2 | a gate block | the passage and its ambush, the murder-hole floor above it as a real reachable storey, the portcullis chamber and winch, the porter's guardroom, the roof and the Gatewright, the drain channel |
| Z3 | a flooded ward | the causeway with its two weed pinches, two arcades climbable end to end as the upper route, the water-gate tower's three storeys, the wrecks, and at the Dead Ebb the whole floor as new ground |
| Z4 | a roofless cloister | four arcade walks, the three stations of Ide's round, the collapsed canopy as broken cover, the rubble the corpse-ambush hides in, the banded door, the hour-vault beneath |
| Z5 | a great hall | the hearth as arena cover, the tapestry sequence read panel by panel along the wall, the roof-post grid Ridd is fought around, the far arch and its chairs, Ancel's chamber, the keep stair |
| Z6 | the largest interior in the delve | ranked piers making the plan legible, the collapse-shaft's rubble cone as climbable terrain, the supply channel as a lethal line across the floor, the Choir's echo vault, the well head and its apron, the grille, and the silt bed the Dead Ebb uncovers |
| Z7 | a tower | the ramp approach, the tower foot, the broken flight and its rope crossing, the ringing floor, the louvre stage, and a belfry sized to be walked around a bell that is itself a building |
