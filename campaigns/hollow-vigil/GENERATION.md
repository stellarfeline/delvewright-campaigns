## Notable decisions

- **Single `pool/stone-keep` area, pieces 10–13** — the only pool in the
  library; two 1-socket required terminals (small-a strongbox, boss-hall) force
  the v0.3 branching solver.
- **Both homages carried by one NPC** (Hedric): the knee wound explains why he
  can't come along, which motivates the shield gift — the two references
  reinforce each other instead of sitting in separate corners.
- **Boss in `keep-boss-hall`, altar in `keep-shrine`**: the solver placed the
  shrine as a branch cap even though the DSL never references it, and the
  ambiguous `anchor/objective` (defined by both shrine and boss-hall) resolved
  to the shrine. Rather than fight the resolution, the narrative was adapted:
  the First Warden falls at his post, then the player carries the ending to the
  barrow altar. Compiler follow-ups filed from this: ambiguous-anchor
  diagnostics, and role-aware capping (a duplicate spawn-hall was also placed
  as a cap).
- **Seed 38 at pieces 10–15**: `keep-boss-hall` (11×13) fails DW0304 on most
  seeds (3/40 solvable at max 15, 0/17 at max 13) — large-terminal placement
  is a known solver robustness gap; the seed was chosen by sweep.
- **`minecraft:trial_key` as the barrow key** — reads as "Trial Key" in-game,
  closest vanilla item to a quest key without NBT.
- **Boss tuned for solo play**: 1 wither skeleton + 1 zombie add; the garrison
  wave is 3 zombies. Iron-kit classes keep both fights winnable alone.
- **Flag chain**: warned → vigil-sworn → halls-quiet → gate-open → warden-risen
  → vigil-broken; every dynamic is authored, nothing spawns uncued.
