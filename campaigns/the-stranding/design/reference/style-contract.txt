The Stranding: every image in this series is one place drawn in the same hand — a Minecraft Java Edition build shown as a high-quality in-game screenshot with a soft night shader. Every surface is made of visible one-metre cubic blocks with vanilla Minecraft textures (cobblestone, stone bricks, spruce and dark oak planks and logs, deepslate, mud and packed mud, black and grey concrete, prismarine, lanterns, glass panes), crisp block edges, no smooth curves anywhere: round or organic forms are stepped block shapes. The place is a grey slate-roofed fishing town on cliff terraces above a bay, a plain of wet black mud risen from the bay, and a vast stranded sea creature built of dark blocks lying at the far edge of the mud. Night under a clear sky and a bright full moon: cold blue-silver moonlight on wet mud and slate roofs, deep blue-black shadows, small warm yellow pools of lantern light at doors and windows, sea water near-black with silver moon glints. Each image is ONE single full-frame view of that place, filling the frame edge to edge: never a sheet, never a grid, never a panel, never an inset, never a caption, no text, no labels, no border, no interface.
