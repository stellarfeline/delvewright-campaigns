# The Stranding — the approved design

This is the approved set. **Author from the image, judge against it, present every choice beside it.**

The map's five reference views (`reference/`) and one near and one far view per scene (`concept/`), every image drawn in one series under the style contract in `reference/style-contract.txt`, anchored on `reference/view1-north`. Each image's sky is its row in `../design.json`. The geometry is `../geometry-brief.json` and `../site-plan.json`; a picture is style authority only.

## Known flaws in the approved set

- `concept/24-jaw-bank-near` puts three pale tablets on the head; the design has one, the Brow Stone in the forehead. The rest of the picture is the body as `../DESIGN.md` § The body states it.
- `concept/21-the-narrows-near` gives the mouth pale lips with an orca's look; the silhouette is otherwise the one body.
- `concept/09-customs-house-near` carries readable lettering on the wall chart, which the style contract forbids; the chart is a prop with no words in the game.
- The body in these images is a whale-like leviathan drawn before the body was ruled a rotting sperm whale (`../DESIGN.md` § The body). The sculpted form follows the record, not these pictures, where they differ.
