# The Still Ones

> **Requires delve engine 0.39.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.12.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

Lantern Row is a short street of doorways in a village at dusk. Someone stands
in every door. They do not speak and they do not move; they only turn to look
at whoever walks past. Reeve Annick waits at the top of the row, and she will
tell you why.

A demo level of about ten minutes, for one or two players. No combat.

## Who you will meet

- **Reeve Annick** keeps the custom of the row, and has for thirty years.
- **The still ones**, in their doors: the tailor's sister, old Brand at the
  cooper's, and the keeper of the watch-house.

## Classes

- **Warder** — a lantern and a shield. The keeper of the watch-house will
  know you.
- **Traveller** — a walking staff and bread. Nobody on this row has seen you
  before.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → `localhost:25565`.

## For the owner: what to look for

This level is where the watch (spec-0101) is looked at in the game. The
machine has proved every turn by yaw; what it cannot say is whether a figure
**reads as watching**. Walk it once as a Warder; then, if a second player is
available, walk it again with one Warder and one Traveller.

1. **Each figure turns to face the nearest player.** Walk up the row slowly.
   The figure in the tailor's door (a pale woman in grey), the dry figure in
   the cooper's door, and — for a Warder — the old man holding a lantern in the watch-house door
   each turn, body and head together, to face you while you are within reach
   (7, 7 and 8 blocks). Stop in front of a door and step from side to side:
   does the figure follow you, and does it read as *watching* rather than as
   a twitch?
2. **It holds its last look.** Walk on past a figure until you are out of its
   reach, then turn round. It should still face the place you were when you
   left its reach — it does not swing back to the street.
3. **The class-only watcher ignores the other class.** As a Traveller, walk
   past the watch-house door: the keeper does not turn to you at all. With a
   Warder and a Traveller together, he turns to the Warder even when the
   Traveller is nearer.
4. **The walking figure stops watching while it walks, and watches again
   where it stops.** The reeve stands at the top of the row facing you. When
   you come within 7 blocks she turns and walks up into the square, round the
   well; while she walks she faces where she is going, not you. Where she
   stops, beyond the well, she turns to face the nearest player again.
5. **Two players: the figure chooses between them.** Walk side by side past a
   door, then let one player drop back. The figure faces whichever of you is
   nearer, and changes when you cross over.

Speak to the reeve at the well to finish.
