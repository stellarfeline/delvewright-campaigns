# The Tidewatch — approved design set

These four images are the approved reference for this campaign. Author from the image, judge against it, present every choice beside it.

Two scenes, each with a near view and a far view. All four are one series: view 1 (`shore-far`) was drawn from its prompt alone and the other three were anchored on it, so every sidecar carries the same interaction id and the three later ones record view 1's id at `request.chain_from`.

| name | scene | view | what it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shore-far.jpg` | the shore | far | the Tidewatch on its headland from off the water, lamp window dark, the tide stair dropping to the landing and the cave mouth beside it |
| `shore-near.jpg` | the shore | near | eye height on the stair itself, looking into the sea cave, the oil cask wedged in the rocks, one corner of the tower's stonework overhead |
| `tower-far.jpg` | the tower | far | the climb as a cutaway: entry hall, the stair through three floors, the lamp room at the top |
| `tower-near.jpg` | the tower | near | eye height in the lamp room, the cold lamp on its plinth against the open seaward window |

The style note held constant across the series: a cold night palette of wet black rock, grey weathered stone and a green-black sea, painted concept art with visible brushwork, moonlight as the only warm value, no saturated colour, and each image one single full-frame view.

Reference imagery is style authority and never dimensional authority. Nothing here fixes a block count, an extent or a piece; the geometry is what the prefab pools assemble.
