# The Reckoning

> **Requires delve engine 0.46.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.13.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

Once a year Wend Mere is let out through its sluice, and the Tallyhouse in its
bed stands dry for a day. Its keeper, Ysolde Marrow, reckons the house before
the water comes back. This year she has asked strangers to walk it first: to
the callbell, to the man in the cell, to the ledger on its stand, and to do
with each what they think right.

A demo level of about ten minutes, for one to four players. No combat.

## Who you will meet

- **Ysolde Marrow** keeps the Tallyhouse, as her mother did before her. She
  sent for you, and she is waiting by the altar at the back of the house.
- **Tobin Reave** is chained in the cell off the porch, held there against his
  family's debt until the reckoning.

## Classes

- **Witness** — Ysolde's summons and a lantern. You walk the Tallyhouse before
  it is reckoned, and what you do there is what the keeper reckons.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → `localhost:25565`. Accept the
resource pack when the server offers it.

## For the owner: what to look for

This level is where an ending that reads the whole run (spec-0108) is looked
at in the game. Three things in the house are yours to do or leave — ring the
callbell in the porch, free Tobin or leave him chained, burn the ledger in the
brazier beside its stand — and Ysolde reckons the house by all three. Three
endings follow: **the house burns** if the ledger burned; otherwise **the house
stands** if you rang the bell or freed Tobin, and **the house floods** if you
did neither. Play it more than once, choosing differently each time.

1. **Does the keeper's speech name what you did in each thread?** When you tell
   Ysolde to reckon the house, she takes the bell, then Tobin, then the ledger,
   one line each, and each line should say exactly what you did with it — rang
   it or not, freed him or not, burned it or not. After the camera returns, one
   closing line per thread says where it ended (the valley on the mud or at
   home; Tobin home, still chained, or let out by Ysolde).
2. **Does each ending follow from what you chose?** Her verdict comes from the
   tally and the deed: burning the ledger ends the house in fire whatever else
   you did; ringing the bell or freeing Tobin keeps it standing; doing neither
   floods it. Note whether the verdict reads as a consequence of your choices
   or as arbitrary.
3. **Does each aftermath read as that ending when the camera returns?**
   - *The house burns*: you (and Ysolde) stand out on the mud before the door,
     and the sanctum floor is on fire behind the arch.
   - *The house stands*: you stand in the sanctum and the candles along the
     altar are lit.
   - *The house floods*: you stand in the sanctum with the mere's water over
     the floor, running out through the arch.
