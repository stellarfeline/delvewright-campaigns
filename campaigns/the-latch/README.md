# The Latch

> **Requires delve engine 0.65.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.14.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

Old Wenna's cottage stands at the end of the lane with her garden locked
behind it. She has left the latch for whoever comes: through her yard gate,
through the kitchen and the oak door to the back room, and the iron grille to
the garden opens when the latch is pulled.

A demo level of five minutes or so, for one to four players. No fight.

## Classes

- **Neighbour** — a walking stick and bread. Wenna asked a neighbour to mind
  her cottage and open her garden.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → the address the host gives you.
Accept the resource pack when the server offers it.

## For the owner: what to look for

This demo level shows one thing: a door is a door. Every wooden door,
trapdoor and gate a player meets opens by hand in adventure mode, and an iron
one does not. Walk it once as the neighbour.

1. **The yard gate.** A wooden gate stands in the hedge across the yard, on
   the path to the cottage. Does it open with a right-click, and can you walk
   through?
2. **The front door and the oak door between the rooms.** The cottage's front
   door, and the oak door from the kitchen to the back room, are both closed
   when you arrive. Do they open by hand, and close again?
3. **The trapdoors.** In the back room's floor, by the hay, three oak
   trapdoors lie over a short stone stair down to the cellar. Do they open by
   hand, and can you go down and come back up?
4. **The iron door.** In the garden's far hedge stands an iron door to the
   lane. Does a right-click do nothing to it, before and after the latch?
5. **The grille.** The iron grille from the back room to the garden is shut
   until you pull the latch lever on the back room's floor. Is it shut before
   the lever, and open after?

The machine has already confirmed: the walk counts every wooden door, wooden
trapdoor and fence gate as a door a body opens, and an iron door or iron
trapdoor as a wall; the bot's run crossed the yard gate, the front door and
the oak door closed, opening each by hand. One change from the queue row: the
way out is the iron grille, not an iron door, because the engine cannot yet
make a two-high door the bar of a shut way; the iron door stands in the far
hedge instead.

## 给主人看的：要看什么

这个演示关卡只展示一件事：门就是门。玩家碰到的每一扇木门、木活板门和栅栏门，在冒险模式下都能用手打开；铁做的不能。以邻居的身份走一遍即可。

1. **院门。** 院子里横着一道树篱，通往小屋的小路上有一扇木栅栏门。右键能打开吗？能走过去吗？
2. **前门和两屋之间的橡木门。** 你到的时候，小屋的前门和厨房通往里屋的橡木门都是关着的。用手能打开、再关上吗？
3. **活板门。** 里屋地上、干草捆旁边，三块橡木活板门盖着一段通往地窖的小石阶。用手能打开吗？能下去再上来吗？
4. **铁门。** 花园最里面的树篱上有一扇通往小巷的铁门。拉门闩之前和之后，右键它都没有反应吗？
5. **铁栅门。** 里屋通往花园的铁栅门，在你拉下里屋地上的门闩拉杆之前是关着的。拉之前关着、拉之后开了吗？

机器已经确认：行走模型把每一扇木门、木活板门和栅栏门都当作身体能打开的门，把铁门和铁活板门当作墙；机器人那一趟是在关着的状态下穿过院门、前门和那扇橡木门的，每一扇都是用手打开的。和队列里那一行有一处不同：出口是铁栅门而不是铁门，因为引擎现在还不能把两格高的门做成一条关闭通路的栅栏；铁门改放在了花园最里面的树篱上。
