# The Beached Thing

> **Requires delve engine 0.38.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.12.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

A short walk around, into and over a body the size of a hill: a sperm whale a hundred and forty blocks long, stranded and rotting on a mud flat in a valley floor.

## What you will do

You arrive on the mud beside a wound in the whale's west flank, where the hide is torn away and white ribs show through.

- **Look at it first.** Climb the mud bank that stands off its west flank. From the top, the whole body lies in view, from the square head to the flukes laid flat on the mud.
- **Go in.** The wound lets you into the body. The first room inside is lit by soul lanterns; a passage leads on to a second room, lit by crying obsidian.
- **Get on top.** A stair cut up the east flank brings you out onto the whale's back.

Reaching the back ends the delve.

## Before you start

Pick a class when you join: the **Wanderer** carries a blade, boots, bread and a draught of night vision; the **Warder** trades reach for a shield, an axe and two healing flasks. Nothing on the flat is hostile, so either will do.
