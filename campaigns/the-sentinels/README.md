# The Sentinels

> **Requires delve engine 0.60.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.13.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

The Sentinel Hall at Varrow keeps the figures of the old watch: armoured dead
set on stone plinths along a red carpet, each where it fell asleep on its post.
The keeper, Ama Strell, lets visitors walk the hall to the far door and touch
what they like. The figures do not wake. Nearly all of them.

A demo level of about ten minutes, for one to four players.

## Who you will meet

- **Ama Strell** keeps the Sentinel Hall and has dusted every figure in it for
  twenty years. She meets visitors in the lodge at the hall's north end.

## Classes

- **Visitor** — a road sword, a travelling coat and some bread. You came to see
  the old watch.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → `localhost:25565`. Accept the
resource pack when the server offers it.

## For the owner: what to look for

This level is where a harmless body saying what it is (spec-0119) is looked at
in the game. A figure of a kind the game calls a monster, that cannot be harmed
and does not fight, standing where the party walks, answers a blow — and, an
actor, a right-click — with one line the campaign wrote, on the action bar of
the player who struck it, to that player alone. The machine has proved the
census: eight bodies in the hall, six of them of a kind the game calls a
monster; the four statues and the captain each owe and carry a line for a blow
and one for a touch; the iron golem carries two it does not owe (the game keeps
golems on peaceful); the dry figure by the far door owes none, because a blow
on it already does something; and each line is dispatched as the player who
pressed. The bot walks the hall and strikes nothing. What the machine cannot
say is how any of it **reads**. Walk it once as a Visitor, and hit things the
way you would.

1. **Strike and right-click every figure on the plinths.** There are four
   statues before the captain, two on each side of the carpet. Each answers a
   blow with one line and a right-click with another, each in its own register
   — dust, cold, a warning, a joke. Does the line read at the crosshair before
   it fades? Do you stop hitting after one line, or keep testing?
2. **No sound comes with the line yet.** The engine has no refusal cue at this
   build, so the answer is the line alone. Does a silent line read as the
   statue answering, or as the game ignoring you?
3. **The iron golem halfway down, on the floor beside the carpet.** It answers
   too, though nothing makes it owe a line. Does its box feel like the golem
   (it is wider and taller than the statues), and does a blow anywhere on the
   golem get its line?
4. **The captain is the last figure on your left as you walk down**, in full
   iron with his sword drawn. Strike him before you reach the far door: he
   answers like the others. Walk on to the door: when you reach it the captain
   steps down off his plinth and comes for you. Strike him then: he bleeds and
   fights, and says nothing. Does his waking read as a payoff after a hall of
   statues?
5. **The dry figure leaning by the far door.** Strike it: it jerks off the
   wall, rattles and settles back, and its line is the blow's own. Right-click
   it: it is a character, so it shows its own short dialogue instead of a line.
6. **With a second player:** stand beside a friend while they strike a statue.
   You should read nothing; only the player who struck sees the line.

Reach the far door to finish.
