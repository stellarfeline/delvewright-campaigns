# The Ferry Bell — generation record

- **What the brief pinned**: the demo-level row for spec-0104 — one shore, a
  jetty and a ferry house; a talk, a sign to read, a lever that opens the ferry
  house, a bell that calls a fight forming up in sight across the water; every
  title in both languages; the `teaching` block in the keeper's voice; no
  marker anywhere; built once with the lever among its rail's fence posts,
  refused, then moved.
- **What was invented**: the keeper (Nell Garrow) and her bad arm, the drowned
  on the spit, the rain, the notice's wording, the outro.
- **Engine**: an unreleased engine revision, built from source (Init I3b, dev
  mode) — `delvec 1.13.0, dsl 0.42.0, mc 1.21.11` from engine branch
  `feature/guidance-can-be-followed` at
  `4dd07d8cad304d9623daef6870b631732adbd726`. The released `delvec 1.13.0`
  states the same version number with `dsl 0.40`-line documents; this level
  does not build with it.
- **Design gate**: not held. A demo level is confirmed by its owner on the
  finished thing; no reference images were drawn and no `design.json` exists,
  so the staging gate's two design-record findings stay red.
- **Posture**: functional text first. Every title opens with its kind's verb;
  hints give direction by what the player can already see.
