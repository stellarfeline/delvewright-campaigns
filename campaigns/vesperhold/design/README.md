# Vesperhold — approved design set

Author from the image, judge against it, present every choice beside it.

The layout of record is `programs/castle/layout.py`; these images are the authority on look and mood, never on position. Where an image and the layout disagree, the layout wins.

## Known departures from the written design

- `concept/armory-near` draws the false chest as a toothed wooden box; in the delve it is a shulker.
- `reference/view3-west` draws the shelf as a low wall under the curtain; it is a shelf on the cliff face.
- `reference/view2-plan` runs the side route down the west slope instead of along the south cliff to the gatehouse.
- `concept/chapel-of-hours-near` draws the shard as a whole small bell.
- `concept/throne-hall-far` is an interior high view, not an aerial one.
