# The Front and the Fountain

> **Requires delve engine 0.66.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.15.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

The town warden walks Tanner's Row before the fair: the new smithy front, the
fountain square, the old stall and the old well-yard at the end of the row.

A test level of five minutes or so, for one to four players. No fight.

## Classes

- **Town Warden** — a rod and bread. Walking the row to judge its fronts and
  its water.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → the address the host gives you.
Accept the resource pack when the server offers it.

## For the owner: what to look for

This test level shows one thing: a building is built in one style — the parts
of a front belong to one construction, and a landmark reads as what it is.
Two good shapes and the two shapes that were judged bad stand side by side on
one row. Look at each from the row first, before you read anything.

1. **The new smithy.** Its street side is open. Behind the front's first row
   stands the frame the wall would have been: a post at each end and between
   the bays, a beam across the whole front two courses up, wall over the beam.
   The counter stands under the beam and the door between two posts, under the
   same head. Do the front's parts belong to one construction?
2. **The fountain square.** In its middle a bowl of water stands raised on a
   stack over a basin of water. Is it a fountain? The water stands in the bowl;
   it does not fall — the engine refuses running water in a piece.
3. **The old stall.** The shape that read badly: a one-course counter and a
   two-course door side by side, with nothing over either under the five-course
   open face. Which part jars?
4. **The old well-yard.** The other bad shape: a flat ring of stone round one
   cell of water at the ring's own height. What is it?

The warden's lodge across the row is only somewhere to stand; it is not one of
the things judged.

The machine has already confirmed: the census names the stall's door as the
one unframed door on the row (`doors framed: 1 of 2`); every place's
threshold is drawn as a frame from the row (ten, one per open side); the bot
walked the row and stood in all four places.

## 给主人看的：要看什么

这个测试关卡只展示一件事：一座建筑用同一种风格建造——门面上的各个部件属于同一种构造，地标看起来就是它自己。两种好的形状和两种被判为不好的形状并排立在同一条街上。先站在街上看每一处，再去读任何说明。

1. **新铁匠铺。** 临街一面敞开。在门面第一排后面，立着原本墙该在的那副框架：两端和两个开间之间各有一根柱子，一根横梁横跨整个门面、离地两层高，横梁上面是墙。柜台在横梁下面，门在两根柱子之间，也在同一根横梁下。门面上的这些部件属于同一种构造吗？
2. **喷泉广场。** 广场中间，一个水碗架在石柱上，下面是一圈水池。它是喷泉吗？水停在碗里，不往下流——引擎不允许零件里有流动的水。
3. **旧摊铺。** 被判为不好的形状：一层高的柜台和两层高的门并排立着，上面什么都没有，头顶是五层高的敞开门面。哪个部件显得突兀？
4. **旧井院。** 另一个不好的形状：一圈平的石头围着一格水，水和石圈一样高。它是什么？

街对面看守人的小屋只是个落脚的地方，不在要评判的东西里。

机器已经确认：普查把旧摊铺的门认作这条街上唯一没有框住的门（`doors framed: 1 of 2`）；每个地方的门槛视角都从街上画成了画面（十张，每面敞开的边一张）；机器人走完了这条街，四个地方都站进去过。
