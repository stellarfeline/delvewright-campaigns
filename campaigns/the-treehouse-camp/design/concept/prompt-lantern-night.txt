Lantern Night: the same camp at night, seen from the Crown Lookout, the small railed plank ring at the very top of the Watch Tree, looking down toward the north-west over the whole camp. Below and in front, the Loom Tree is cut flat on top with its open platform and lean-to; to the left and farther off, the Hearth Tree is the widest tree with the broadest, highest platform, its stone hearth glowing, its huge chunky crown above it; far back on the left the Seed Tree stands lower under its own smaller crown. Straight rope bridges join the platforms. Every platform rail and every rope bridge is outlined by a line of small warm lanterns, so the camp is drawn in gold lines of light in the dark forest, and warm firelight glows from the hearth and the cabins. A dark blue night sky with stars and no moon shows between the chunky leaf crowns. The forest of ordinary giant trees fades into darkness all round. In the near foreground, the ring's plank rail and a single newly hung lantern on an iron hook, lit.
