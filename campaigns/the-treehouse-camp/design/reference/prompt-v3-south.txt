View 3, the same camp seen from the south, from far out on the forest floor at about the height of the platforms, in late afternoon; west is to the LEFT of the frame, east to the RIGHT. The ground is lowest at the left, in a dell, rises toward a hollow near the middle-left, and is highest at the back right. Front left: the Seed Tree growing from the low dell, its square platform about sixteen blocks above its roots. Behind it, middle left: the Hearth Tree, the widest trunk, its broad platform a little higher than the Seed platform, with a straight rope bridge coming forward and gently downhill from it to the Seed platform, and a rope ladder hanging down its trunk to a glade among its roots. Front right: the Watch Tree, the tallest, growing from middle-height ground; its platform with a sentry cabin is about twenty blocks up, the highest platform except one, and a very long rope ladder climbs its trunk from that platform up through its leaves to a small ring of planks at its very top, which stands above every other crown. Back right: the Loom Tree on the highest ground, a slimmer giant, its platform with cloth lines; a straight rope bridge runs from it forward and gently uphill to the Watch platform, and another straight rope bridge runs from it left, across the back, gently downhill to the Hearth platform. All four platforms stand at visibly different heights and the four trees grow from ground at visibly different heights. Chunky leaf crowns above, with gaps of sky between them.
