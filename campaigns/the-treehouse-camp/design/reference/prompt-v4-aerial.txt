View 4, the same camp seen from high in the north-east, looking down toward the south-west at about forty-five degrees, in late afternoon, with the whole camp in frame. Nearest, in the centre foreground: the Loom Tree, its square platform with a lean-to and cloth lines under its crown. From the Loom platform a straight rope bridge runs to the right and away, across a gap of open air, to the Hearth Tree in the middle distance on the right, the widest tree with the broadest platform and a long thatched cabin. From the Loom platform a second straight rope bridge runs to the left and away to the Watch Tree in the middle distance on the left, the tallest tree, whose small plank lookout ring at its very top pokes above its own leaves and stands higher than every other crown, nearly level with the viewer. Far back in the centre, beyond the Hearth Tree, the Seed Tree stands lower in a dell, joined to the Hearth platform by a third straight rope bridge. The chunky leaf crowns do not close: there are gaps of open sky and sunlit air between them, through which the bridges and the mossy forest floor far below can be seen. The forest of ordinary giant trees continues to the horizon all round.
