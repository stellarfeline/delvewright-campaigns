View 2, the same camp seen from straight above, a top-down plan view with north at the top of the frame, in late afternoon. The leafy crowns are cut away, as if lifted off, so the platforms, cabins, bridges and trunks are clearly visible from directly above; only a thin ring of chunky leaves marks the edge of each crown. Four giant trees, each a big round blocky trunk seen from above, each wrapped by a square plank platform. Upper left of centre: the Hearth Tree, the biggest, with the biggest square platform, a long thatched cabin and a stone hearth. Upper right: the Loom Tree, with a smaller square platform, a lean-to and cloth lines. Lower left: the Seed Tree, with a small square platform, baskets and a storehouse cabin. Lower right: the Watch Tree, with a medium square platform and a small sentry cabin, and directly over the middle of it a smaller square ring of planks, the lookout, around the top of the trunk. Three straight narrow rope bridges, each running in a perfectly straight line, either exactly left-right or exactly up-down: one runs straight right from the Hearth platform to the Loom platform; one runs straight down from the Hearth platform to the Seed platform; one runs straight down from the Loom platform to the Watch platform. There is no bridge between the Seed Tree and the Watch Tree. Between the trees, far below, the uneven forest floor of moss, ferns, roots and a few fallen logs, darker in the low dell around the Seed Tree at the lower left and on higher ground at the upper right.
