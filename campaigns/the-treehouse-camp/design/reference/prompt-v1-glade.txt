View 1, from the forest floor at the foot of the Hearth Tree, looking up and ahead, in late afternoon. The eye stands in a shaded glade among huge blocky buttress roots, each root taller than a person, with ferns and moss underfoot and a ring of stones around a small fire. Just ahead and to the left rises the Hearth Tree, the widest of the giant trees, its trunk as wide as a house. About sixteen blocks overhead a broad square plank platform wraps all the way round its trunk, carrying a long thatched cabin built against the bark and a stone hearth with a fire; hanging lanterns on chains under its rail. A rope ladder hangs straight down the bark of the trunk from a hole in that platform to the roots beside the viewer. From the platform's right-hand side a long narrow rope bridge of planks with post-and-rope rails runs to the right and gently uphill, about twenty blocks, to a smaller square platform with a lean-to roof and cloth hanging on lines, wrapped round a slimmer giant tree that grows from higher ground on the right. Far behind on the right, in the haze, the tallest giant tree: a small cabin platform about twenty blocks up its trunk, and at its very top a small ring of planks poking up just above its own leaves and above all the other crowns. Above everything, the giant trees' chunky leaf crowns, with open gaps of sky between them where sun shafts fall through. The forest floor is uneven, rising to the right and falling away to the left. Every rope bridge is straight in plan, running in a straight line between two platforms.
