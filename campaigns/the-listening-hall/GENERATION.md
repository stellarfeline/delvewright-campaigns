# The Listening Hall — generation record

- **What the brief pinned**: the row of `docs/demo-levels.md` for spec-0100 — a sealed hall whose floor is set with sensors, a shrieker with `can_summon=false` answering from the dark, a calibrated sensor at the far door that hears from sixteen blocks with its input side clear, one story beat turning the floor to sculk (`fill-region` `minecraft:sculk`, `particle` `sculk_charge_pop`/`sculk_soul`, `play-sound` `block.sculk_catalyst.bloom`), and a catalyst sealed in a glass well too deep for any body to reach.
- **What was invented**: the three-room line (antechamber, hall, alcove), the hall's size (9 × 25), the sensor rows along the walls, the shrieker niche in the west wall, the well at the hall's centre, the bloom at the far door, and every line of text.
- **Placement**: a site plan with a solid deepslate fill. The hall is a place the party walks through, and no library piece is a sealed hall with sculk in it; the sculk, the well and the lanterns are laid by `world-edits.json` over the detailed rooms.
- **Engine**: `delvec 1.12.0, dsl 0.39.0`, built from the engine's `integrate/sculk-sound-sight` branch at `6ee70c1483c76d15677400624491ed98ffd0c8cd` (spec-0100 is carried by no release yet).
- **Languages**: English only.
- **Posture**: the hall is a thing that listens, not a thing that hunts. Nothing in it hurts the party; the text never says what the hall is, only what it does.
- **Design gate**: not held. This level was built from its demo-levels row in a run with no one to approve a design walkthrough, so `design/` carries no concept art, `design.json` does not exist and there is no `design/cameras.json`. The staging gate reads that as its two design-record rows UNBOUND, and the written-world comparison (`delvec cameras`, then `check-written-world.py`) has no camera record to draw its at-load world from.
- **Light**: placed by hand with the rooms (`world-edits.json` batch `batch/lanterns`, first in the batch order because the dark-cell proof runs after every batch). Thirteen soul lanterns at y +3 in two staggered rows four blocks apart give a hand-computed darkest walkable cell of light 3 along the walls.
- **Detail**: every place is detailed from `programs/<place>.json`, written by `design/programs-src/rooms.py` from `delvec allocation`; re-run it and `delvec detail . --all` after any plan edit.

## Findings ledger

1. A `fill-region` box is anchor-centred (`anchor ± extent`) and every site-plan anchor stands on the walk plane, so no `fill-region` can write the floor course alone. The row's floor turning to sculk is written as `sculk_vein[down=true]` at walk height.
2. The completability model classifies a `fill-region` of any non-fluid block as solid (`RegionWrite::of_block` reads only `is_fluid`), so a `fill-region` of `sculk_vein`, whose collision shape is empty, is proved as a raised floor. With the bloom at the well, `DW0921` refused the antechamber as a place a body gets into and not out of. The bloom moved to the far door, where no leg follows it.
3. The harness refuses a predicted sensor more than 8 blocks from every waypoint of its leg (`harness/src/waypoints.ts`, `parseVibrations`), while the compiler predicts from the leg's dense route cells, and a straight leg exports two waypoints. Leg 0 predicts the sensor at (8200, 63, 8232), distSq 66 to its nearest waypoint, and the bot refuses the export before walking. On a scratch copy with that one sensor moved a block south (distSq 53), the bot passes with every predicted click and shriek heard and `darkness 0, warden 0`.
