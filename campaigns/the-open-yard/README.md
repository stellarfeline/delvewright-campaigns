# The Open Yard

> **Requires delve engine 0.65.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.14.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

Market day comes to Ashford tomorrow. The steward has sent a runner down the
high street to see that everything is ready: the fountain court, the smith's
open front, the covered market, and the steward's own house across the street.

A demo level of five minutes or so, for one to four players. No fight.

## Classes

- **Steward's Runner** — a staff and bread. Sent down the high street the day
  before market day.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → the address the host gives you.
Accept the resource pack when the server offers it.

## For the owner: what to look for

This demo level shows one thing: a place may stand no wall on a side. Each
side of a place is a wall or open, from all four walls down to none. Walk the
street once as the runner.

1. **The fountain court.** The court in the middle of the street's north side
   stands no wall on any side: its front is on the street, and the green runs
   round its other three sides. Does it read as part of the square — ground
   you walk onto from any side — rather than a room?
2. **The smithy's front.** The smithy is roofed and walled on three sides,
   and its street side is open: a counter runs across the room one cell in from
   the front, with the door beside it. Does the front read as a shop a body
   walks up to and leans on, with the smith's room behind the counter?
3. **The covered market.** The market's roof stands on a post at each corner
   and it has no walls at all. Does it read as a building with no walls, its
   stalls under the roof?
4. **The house.** The steward's house is walled all round, for contrast.

The machine has already confirmed: every open side holds nothing a body
cannot be in, from the floor to the top of the place, and is as wide along its
whole run and one cell beyond as the place is long — nine open sides, all
whole; the court's and the market's fronts are crossings onto the street, and
nothing else joins two places; the bot walked the street, into the smithy
through its door and out again, under the market, and into the house.

## 给主人看的：要看什么

这个演示关卡只展示一件事：一个地方的某一面可以不立墙。每个地方的四面各自可以是墙，也可以敞开，从四面墙到一面墙都没有都行。以跑腿的身份沿街走一遍即可。

1. **喷泉小院。** 街北边正中的小院四面都没有墙：前面是大街，另外三面是绕着它的草地。它读起来是不是广场的一部分——从哪一面都能走上去的地面——而不是一个房间？
2. **铁匠铺的门面。** 铁匠铺有屋顶，三面有墙，临街那一面敞开：离门面一格的地方横着一条柜台，门就在柜台旁边。这个门面读起来是不是一家能走上前、靠在柜台上的铺子，铁匠的屋子在柜台后面？
3. **集市棚。** 集市棚的屋顶由四个角上的柱子撑着，一面墙都没有。它读起来是不是一座没有墙的建筑，摊位都在屋顶下面？
4. **管事的家。** 管事的家四面都是墙，用来对照。

机器已经确认：每一面敞开的边，从地面到这个地方的顶，都没有身体进不去的东西；它整段都和这个地方一样长、一样宽，往外再一格也是——一共九面敞开的边，全部完整；小院和集市棚的门面是通往大街的通道，除此之外没有别的地方把两个地方连在一起；机器人走完了整条街，从门进了铁匠铺又出来，走到集市棚底下，最后进了管事的家。
