View 1, standing in the garden just inside its east gate, at eye height, looking due west along the garden's long axis at sunset, the low sun ahead and slightly to the left. Straight ahead runs a long, narrow rectangular reflecting pool of perfectly still water, five blocks wide and eighteen long, edged in pale stone, mirroring the gold and rose sky; a pale gravel path runs along each side of it, with fresh lawn and low clipped hedges beyond. On the right, against the tall north brick wall, stands the glass conservatory: a long white-framed glasshouse twenty blocks long and ten deep, its walls and its steep pitched roof all clear glass panes in white frames, the setting sun shining through it, green leaves and a small lemon tree visible inside; to its right, nearer the viewer, a small red-brick potting room with a plank door and warm lantern light in its little window. Four round fruit trees in leaf stand in the lawn, two on each side of the pool. The garden is enclosed by red brick walls about four blocks high with stone copings; at the far west end of the pool, the wall rises to a small raised stone terrace with a balustrade, reached by a short flight of stone steps, standing against the open sunset sky. Iron lanterns on short stone posts stand along the paths, not yet lit.
