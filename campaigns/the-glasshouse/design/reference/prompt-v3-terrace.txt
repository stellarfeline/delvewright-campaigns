View 3, standing on the small raised stone terrace on the garden's west wall, at eye height, looking due west, away from the garden, at sunset. In the foreground, a waist-high pale stone balustrade with two stone urns, and a plank bench. Beyond and below it, open rough grassland runs level and far away westward, with a few scattered trees, for a very long distance, to a low line of soft blue-grey hills on the far horizon. The sun is just touching the horizon over those far hills, and the sky is wide and deep: pale gold and rose at the horizon rising to clear deep blue overhead, with a thin crescent moon. A long sense of distance and air, the far hills paler and bluer than the near ground.
