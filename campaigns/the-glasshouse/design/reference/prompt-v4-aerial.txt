View 4, the whole site seen from high in the air to the south-east, looking north-west and down at about forty-five degrees, at sunset. The whole walled garden is in frame with open grass all round it: the rectangle of red brick walls, the long reflecting pool down its middle catching the gold sky, the four fruit trees and the clipped hedges, the long white-framed glass conservatory against the far north wall glowing with the low sun shining through it, the small brick potting room to its right with a red tile roof and a warm lit window, the small raised stone terrace on the far west wall, and in the near right the short avenue of trees leading to the brick gateway in the east wall. Beyond the garden, open level grassland stretches away to low blue hills on the horizon under a gold and rose sunset sky.
