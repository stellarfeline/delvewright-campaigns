View 2, the same garden seen from straight above, a top-down plan view with north at the top of the frame, at sunset with long shadows falling to the right. A rectangle of red brick walls, forty blocks wide and thirty deep, fills the middle of the frame. In its centre a long narrow reflecting pool, eighteen by five, runs left to right, mirroring the sky, with a gravel path along each long side and a cross path at each end; lawn and clipped hedges fill the four quarters, with one round fruit tree in each quarter. Along the north wall, set left of centre, the glass conservatory seen from above: a long rectangle of glass roof panes in white frames with a ridge running left to right, twenty long and ten deep, standing just outside the garden's north wall with a glass door into the garden. To its right, a small brick potting room with a red tile roof, ten by eight, also against the north wall, with a door into the garden. On the left, outside the middle of the west wall, a small square stone terrace eight by eight, with a short stone stair down into the garden. On the right, outside the middle of the east wall, a brick gateway with two lantern piers opens onto a short avenue of trees fourteen long running to the right edge. Outside the walls, open rough grass all round.
