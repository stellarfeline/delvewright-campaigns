Scene: the conservatory, far view. A frontal elevation view: the camera stands on the lawn inside the garden, fifteen blocks south of the conservatory, at eye height, facing due north, square-on to the conservatory's glass front. NOT looking along the pool: the pool is out of frame behind the camera. The whole twenty-block lean-to glasshouse fills the middle of the frame side to side, its glass front and its glass roof sloping up to the tall red brick north wall behind it, white frames, a low brick plinth, a double glass door in the middle with a gravel path coming straight toward the camera. The low western sun comes from the left of the frame and makes the glass glow gold; the lemon tree and greenery show through. On the right edge, the small brick potting room. Lawn in the foreground.
