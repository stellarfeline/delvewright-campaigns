Scene: inside the potting room, near view, in the evening. A small brick room with an oak plank floor and long oak plank benches crowded with clay pots, seed trays, twine and tools; wooden seed drawers on the wall; a hanging iron lantern over the bench giving warm light, a cluster of lit candles on a shelf and on the window sill, a wall torch by a square hatch in the floor with a stone stair going down into darkness. The small window shows the gold evening sky. An older woman in a gardener's apron, her right hand bandaged, stands by the bench looking toward the door. The room is lit only by the lantern, the candles and the torch; deep warm shadows in the corners.
