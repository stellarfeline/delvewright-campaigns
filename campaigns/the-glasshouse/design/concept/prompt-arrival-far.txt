Scene: the lime walk, far view. Seen from about twelve blocks up and twenty blocks east of the avenue, looking west at sunset: the short avenue of six lime trees on a gravel path across rough grass leads to the brick gateway with two lantern piers in the east wall of the walled garden; beyond the wall the long reflecting pool, the fruit trees and the glass conservatory against the north wall are visible, and beyond the garden open grassland runs to low hills under a gold sky.
