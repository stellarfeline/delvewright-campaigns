Scene: the belvedere, far view. From the open grassland about twenty-five blocks west of the garden, at eye height, looking east at sunset: the long red brick west wall of the walled garden, warm in the low sun, with the small raised stone terrace standing out from its middle, its balustrade and urns catching the light; two small figures stand at the balustrade; behind the wall the top of the glass conservatory's roof glows and the crowns of the fruit trees show.
