Scene: the garden, near view. Standing beside the reflecting pool at its east end, at eye height, looking west at sunset: the long still pool edged in pale stone runs away from the viewer mirroring the gold and rose sky and the dark silhouette of the raised stone terrace at its far end; gravel paths, low clipped hedges and a round fruit tree in leaf in the foreground left; iron lanterns on short stone posts along the path, just lit and glowing warm. On the right the glass conservatory glows with the low sun shining through it.
