Scene: the stove cellar, near view. Standing at the foot of a narrow stone stair in a low cellar of rough stone walls and a stone floor, looking across it: one iron lantern hangs on the wall by the stair and is the only light, throwing a warm pool across the floor and leaving the corners in deep shadow; across the room an old iron furnace with its door open and its fire just lit, glowing orange; a heap of coal and a shovel beside it; iron flue pipes running off through the wall. Very dark beyond the lantern's reach.
