Scene: the belvedere, near view. Standing on the small raised stone terrace on the garden's west wall, at eye height, looking due west at sunset: a pale stone balustrade with two stone urns and an oak plank bench in the foreground; beyond and below, open level grassland with scattered trees runs a very long way to low soft blue-grey hills on the far horizon; the sun sits on the horizon over those hills; the sky shades from pale gold and rose at the horizon to deep clear blue overhead, with a thin crescent moon.
