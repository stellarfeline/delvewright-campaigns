Scene: the lime walk, near view. Standing at eye height on a pale gravel path in a short avenue of lime trees with chunky cubic leaf crowns on square trunks, rough grass either side, looking due west at sunset. Ahead, at the end of the avenue, a brick gateway in a red brick garden wall about four blocks high: two square stone-capped piers each carrying an iron lantern, glowing faintly, and an open oak gate. The low sun sits just above the wall top ahead, throwing long shadows of the trees back toward the viewer; over the wall the white-framed glass roof of a conservatory catches the light.
