Scene: the garden, far view. From high on the south wall's east corner, looking north-west across the whole walled garden at sunset: the reflecting pool down the middle, the four lawn quarters with clipped hedges and fruit trees, the lantern posts glowing along the paths, the glass conservatory and the small brick potting room with its lit window against the tall north wall, and the raised stone terrace on the west wall with the sun setting beyond it.
