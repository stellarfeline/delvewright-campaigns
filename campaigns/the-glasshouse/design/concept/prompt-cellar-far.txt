Scene: the stove cellar, far view. A cutaway section view, as if the ground and the wall were sliced away, seen from the side at dusk: above, the small brick potting room with its benches, its hanging lantern and candles glowing; a narrow stone stair runs down through a hatch in its floor into the low stone cellar below ground, lit by one lantern at the stair foot, with the iron furnace glowing at the far side and flue pipes running off sideways under the glass conservatory next door. Earth and stone around the cellar.
