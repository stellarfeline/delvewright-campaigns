Scene: inside the conservatory, near view. Standing at eye height on a tiled floor inside the lean-to glasshouse, looking west along its length at sunset: the glass roof slopes up to the brick back wall on the right, made of clear square panes in white frames; warm low sunlight streams in through the glass front on the left and falls in long squares of light across oak plank plant staging, clay pots, ferns and climbing vines; in the centre stands an old lemon tree in a big square wooden tub, heavy with yellow lemons; at the near end on the right, an iron winding wheel and rods that open and shut the roof vents. The sky shows through the glass overhead.
