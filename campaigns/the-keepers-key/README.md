# The Keeper's Key

> **Requires delve engine 0.68.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.15.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

Hob Varley keeps the toll house at Varley Ford: a hall with a hearth, the store
behind it, and across the yard the shed where the ford's punt is kept. The store
is locked and its key is at the keeper's belt; the shed gate is worked from a
lever in the store.

A test level of five minutes or so, for one to four players. No fight. Go into
the punt shed to finish.

## Classes

- **Traveller** — road boots, bread and a road flask.

## Playing it

Start the server, then in the Minecraft Java client at the version the marker
above names: Multiplayer → Direct Connect → the address the host gives you.

## For the owner: what to look for

This test level shows one thing: a door the story opens is the door of a real
way. The store door in the hall's wall is shut by the door itself — no bars, no
second block — and the keeper's key is used on that door.

1. **Before the keeper**: walk up to the store door and use it. It says it is
   locked, and it does not open. Can you get into the store any other way?
2. **Speak to the keeper**: he hands you his key. Use the door again without
   the key in your hand: it says the key opens it.
3. **Hold the key and use the door once**: it is unlocked. From then on it
   opens and shuts by hand like any door. Is it the same door you tried, and
   does it open onto a real room — the store, with its hay and sacks and the
   lever on the back wall?
4. **The lever**: it is padlocked; hold the key and use it. The view cuts to
   the shed gate across the yard as its bars go up. Does the shot make the gate
   the thing the lever did, though you cannot see the gate from the store?
5. **The hearth**: rest at the fire in the hall's corner. Do its two buttons
   say what each does?
6. Walk across the yard into the punt shed to finish.
