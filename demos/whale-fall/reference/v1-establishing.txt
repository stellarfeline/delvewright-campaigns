Far establishing view from three-quarters front-left and slightly above: the entire whale skeleton floats over the cloud sea, roughly 130 blocks long, big as a small hill. From left to right: a huge wedge-shaped skull with the two great bowed lower-jaw bones hanging open beneath it, the skull hollow with a small ruined stone temple visible through the eye socket; then the spine running back as a long, gently arched row of vertebrae with tall blade-like spines on top, wide enough to walk along; under the front half of the spine, about fifteen pairs of ribs arch down and outward like a colonnade around an open chest cavity; two paddle-shaped flipper skeletons hang below the chest; the spine tapers into a long tail of shrinking vertebrae ending at a broad horizontal fluke. Tiny figures would be lost at this scale.
