Whale-fall (鲸落), in the same hand throughout: a Minecraft-style voxel world rendered as clean, softly lit concept art; the subject is the bleached skeleton of a colossal dead sky-whale floating motionless above an endless sea of white clouds, under a pale high sky. Bone is chalk-white to warm ivory, built of large square blocks so its stepped edges read as voxel masonry; small ruined grey-stone temple pieces sit inside the skull. Calm, eerie, vast; no creatures, no people, no text. Each image is ONE single full-frame view of that place, filling the frame edge to edge: never a sheet, never a grid, never a panel, never an inset or a caption.
