# The Beached Thing

> **Requires delve engine 0.39.0 or newer** — last verified with delvec 1.13.0 on Minecraft Java 1.21.11.

A body the size of a hill lies beached on a mud flat in a valley floor. A wound in its flank lets a party in, two rooms inside it hold their own light, and a stair cut up its other flank leads onto its back.
